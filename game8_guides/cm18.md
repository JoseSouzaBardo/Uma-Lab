Signups for the Libra Cup (CM18) are expected to begin on **August 25, 2026**, with the first round of races to begin on **August 28, 2026 10PM** (UTC). This is the second Champions Meeting during the [Grand Concert (Grand Live)](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/607337) scenario.

Course Notes
・The **late-race** starts near the exit of the final corner. The acceleration skills [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) will work here. [Budding Blossom](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594184) is also great for Pace. [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881) only lasts long enough for [Taiki Shuttle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536317) herself. 

・The **final straight** is around 60m after the start of the late-race. 

・A **large downhill** starts near the middle of the final corner and extends past the halfway point of the last straight. [Straight Descent](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536993) can be usable acceleration here. 

・There is a **small uphill** near the end of the track, right after the long downhill. Uphill-dependent skills like Moxie will only activate here.

Libra Cup (CM18) will likely see **Christmas Oguri Cap** becoming a dominant force in yet another Champions Meeting. However, she needs a special build as an End Closer, which does require parents with enough sparks. Unlike the [Virgo Cup (CM17)](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/610685), she doesn't have a backup recovery in case one fails to activate, so she's slightly less consistent this time. However, she's still consistently strong enough to run double debuffers as her teammates.

Pace Chasers have the best shot at competing against Christmas Oguri Cap as they have great acceleration options like [Budding Blossom](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594184) and [Neck and Neck](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594185). This style can also gamble on [Determined Descent](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536940) as the downhill is in a decent position.

Front Runners once again have [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) to maintain their lead throughout the race. They also have less consistent gamble options with [Final Push](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536965) and [Second Wind](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537056). As with other CMs where Budding Blossom is strong, running triple Fronts may also make it difficult for Pace Chasers to meet the 3rd-4th position requirement of the skill.

Late Surgers got the most benefit with the July update, and they have several strong rare accelerations to choose from, including [On Your Left!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536943) and [Lightning Surge](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/614078), the rare version of [Leap Forward](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/608322). Unfortunately, only a few of them are truly standouts, with one being Camping Mejiro Dober.

・**2 Fronts + 1 Any** - Front-leaning composition. 2 Fronts guarantee support for each other. 3rd ace can be another Front, a Pace, or End Closer Christmas Oguri Cap.

・**2 Pace + 1 Any** - Pace-leaning composition. 2 Pace makes the challenged condition of Budding Blossom easier.

・**1 Ace + 2 Debuffer** - Team composition for Christmas Oguri Cap. Not recommended for most other aces. [Dominator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922) with [Keen Eye](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536913) is the recommended debuff combo.

・**3 Lates/Ends** - Gambling-focused strategy; backliners are somewhat outmatched here.

However, it's still random, so it can be weaker compared to Lightning Surge.

Under the Grand Concert (Grand Live) scenario, the typical deck setup will have **3 Speed cards**, **2 Wit cards**, and [Light Hello SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/607411). For substitutions, you can trade one Speed or Wit card for a **Power** card only if you specifically want their rare skill such as [Lightning Surge](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/614078) or [Neck and Neck](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594185).

If you're using [Light Hello R](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/607413), it can still be used in a budget deck for the extra performance points, but it will be noticeably weaker than the SSR version. For the budget decks, we highly recommend borrowing a skill card due to the importance of getting strong acceleration in the late-race.
