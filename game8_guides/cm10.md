Course Notes
・Late-race starts on the final corner. This means the strong inheritable acceleration skills, [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), are best here. [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) is potentially strong, but unreliable due to spreading out at the final corner. 

・Some final corner accelerations are only good under certain conditions. For example, [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881) is only good on [Taiki Shuttle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536317) herself. [Red Shift/LP1211-M](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883) is possibly usable by Late/Ends only. [Moving Past, and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534) activates too early to be effective. 

・Final straight isn't too late for acceleration. This means skills like [Victoria por plancha](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543206), [Give Mummy a Hug](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/567567), and [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) are all viable. 

・Downhill is present, so investing in Wit is good. However, do not learn Straight Descent, because it isn't effective even if it activates. 

・Uphill is in the late-race, and could be used for acceleration with [Highlander](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536994). The uphill is also fairly long, so **strong acceleration** and **high Power** is necessary to win.

The Aquarius Cup is the first Dirt Champions Meeting. Much like the previous CM, there aren't a lot of easy training options to choose from, as the Global server is lacking good Dirt racers.

Among them, [El Condor Pasa](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536304) as a Pace Chaser is the **most accessible** and has a decent unique. However, her unique is more reliable as a Front Runner, which will also require multiple sparks. [Agnes Digital](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/566109) is also expected to be **one of the strongest options** here due to having [Lead the Charge](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/567064) and [Superstan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/567063). She works **best as an End Closer**, but can be viable as a Late Surger, too.

Other notable Umamusume include [Smart Falcon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541253) and [Summer Maruzensky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/556411), who are the most viable Front Runners. [Taiki Shuttle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536317) and [Oguri Cap](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536318) are also good Pace options if you have them. The recently released [New Year Haru Urara](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/575508) could also be a competitive racer, especially when paired with another ace that can widen the gap and make her unique stronger. As for the regular [Haru Urara](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536294), she's not as competitive for this race but can be run as a Late Surger if you don't have other options.

Due to the long uphill at the start of the late-race, **strong acceleration** is key to winning this race. [Lead the Charge!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/567064) is a Dirt acceleration for all styles that will become available with the upcoming Agnes Digital Power SSR and is already on [Agnes Digital](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/566109) herself. Its base form, [Forward, March!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/567062), is also a viable alternative since that card is generally underwhelming.

Also, aim to get inheritable uniques, like [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), which are strong on this track. For rare acceleration skills like [Furious Feat](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536918), you can learn its base version from sparks or skill hints instead if it means using a stronger support card for better stats.

[No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) is less reliable in Mile races due to how runners spread out in the final corner. However, it's potentially strong if you're lucky and get it to activate as soon as the late-race starts. We still recommend avoiding it unless you don't have other options for acceleration.

Lastly, don't forget to learn some mid-race velocity skills like [Professor of Curvature](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536923), so your aces can get in good positions before hitting the late-race.

For the Aquarius Cup support cards, aim for **Wit decks** with at least **2 Speed cards** and **ideally 3 Wit cards**. The final card can either be **Power** to help reach the high amount required for this race, or a **Pal** card such as [Riko Kashimoto](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/561497). Some good Power cards include [Daitaku Helios SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572281) for Front Runners and [Admire Vega SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/575513) for End Closers. [Oguri Cap SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536693) can also be considered for Late and End for [Furious Feat](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536918).

As with previous Champions Meetings, aim to get at least [34 Race Bonus](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572831) to gain more stats from winning races. If this isn't possible, try to use high-level cards instead because high uncap SRs can outperform SSRs with no uncaps.
