Course Notes
・Soft and Rainy conditions are a first for Champions Meetings. This means you'll need to compensate for the penalty by increasing **Power**. 

・**Late-race starts on the final corner**. Powerful skills include [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060), [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881), and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235). 

・**Final straight is late in late-race**. Avoid[Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) for this race as it will have a diminished effect. 

・Uphills and downhills are not ideal for acceleration skills. Avoid[Highlander](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536994) and [Straight Descent](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536993), as these will have virtually no effect. 

・ 777m point for [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026) occurs at the **start of the final corner in the mid-race**, close to the 1250m point ~(1220m).

For the Scorpio Cup, we're expecting **Pace Chasers** to be the best style to run, mostly due to the existence of **Runaway Silence Suzuka**, who can block Front Runners. Among the Pace Chasers, [Taiki Shuttle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536317) (with enough Medium sparks) is shaping up to be one of the strongest, thanks to her improved [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881) skill. [Meisho Doto](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/557789) is also looking like a good pick for Pace.

**Christmas Oguri Cap**, which is projected to be released during the period of the Scorpio CM, is also expected be a very strong pick due to her unique skill. Not only does it provide her acceleration and velocity at an optimal point in the race (when triggered at the same time as [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026)), but also gives her recovery, potentially shielding herself from stamina debuffers.

In the Japanese version of the Scorpio Cup, Front Runners were the best option since [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) is very powerful on this track. [Summer Maruzensky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/556411), in particular, was the best choice since she could only realistically be blocked by another Summer Maruzensky. However, with [Silence Suzuka](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536321) being able to learn [Runaway](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/563800) now, this makes it difficult for them to guarantee the lead at the final corner. In lobbies without Silence Suzuka, expect Front Runners to still be tough competition.

Scorpio Cup is notable for having several Umamusume releases in December that can be good for the track. All of them are best run as Late Surgers, so winning can still be a bit of a gamble. [Manhattan Cafe](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/568614) has a speed debuff tied to her unique skill, [Kimono Gold City](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/570623) has another final corner acceleration unique skill, which is suitable for the track, and **Mejiro Dober** has similar strengths to Mejiro Ryan while being able to use [All-Seeing Eyes](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536906).

Scorpio Cup is a Medium Race, so expect to see Debuffers appearing again. Unlike other Medium tracks, this track is shorter at 2,000m, meaning speed debuffs like [Dominator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922) could be more impactful. [Mystifying Murmur](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536891) will likely only cripple unprepared opponents, since it should be easier to hit the stamina requirement. **Hybrid debuffers** that only carry one gold debuff should also be viable here.

As with previous Champions Meetings, acceleration skills that activate as soon as the late-race begins are good. For this race, we want **skills that activate on the final corner**. Inherited skills that are strong are [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) (Front), [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881) (Pace), and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) (Late/End). General acceleration skills like [Slick Surge](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536973) and [Head-On](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564783) can also be learned. End Closers can technically use [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039), but the position of the final straight makes it noticeably weaker.

Upon further testing, [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) is not as good in Scorpio Cup due to how the runners spread out on the final corner. This means it doesn't trigger often. However, if you're lucky, it's potentially one of the best acceleration skills for the track.

Front Runners will always want strong early-race acceleration to secure the lead. [Taking the Lead](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536941) from the recently released [Mihono Bourbon Wit SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/567531) is one of the best options. This can be paired with **[Groundwork](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537044) and green skills**. Having the skill [Concentration](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536926)/[Focus](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536964) is also good to reduce the effects of Late Starts. Combining early-race positioning skills like [Dodging Danger](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537606) and [Prudent Positioning](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536944) is also good for getting ahead, if you can learn both.

**Christmas Oguri Cap**'s unique skill, which is a split **accel, velocity, and recovery skill** and is triggered by activating 3 recovery skills, is most efficient when used with [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026) plus 2 other white recovery skills.

If all of these skills are acquired, gold recovery will not be needed for her in this distance, since the 3 white recoveries and her unique skill itself will provide more than enough stamina recovery than 1 lone gold recovery in total.

This makes Christmas Oguri Cap a **very strong Pace Chaser** in Medium and Long distances, since building towards optimizing her unique skill naturally also shields her from other stamina debuffers.
