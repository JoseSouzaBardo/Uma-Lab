Course Notes
・Late-race starts on the 3rd Corner. The best acceleration skills include [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060), [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), and [Moving Past, and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534). Final corner accelerations ([Red Shift](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883) / [Shooting for Victory](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881)) are not as effective since they activate later. 

・**3 Uphills** and **1 Downhill** mean some investment in **Wit and Power** should help. None are particularly good for acceleration, but recoveries ([Restless](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536911) and [Go-Home Specialist](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536925)) could be used. 

・The [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026) activation point is close to the start late-race. This makes [Christmas Oguri Cap](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572578) very strong because [Festive Miracle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572790) uses both its Speed and Acceleration boosts.

After some not-so-consistent showings from the past 3 CMs, [Christmas Oguri Cap](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572578) should have a high chance of winning Aries Cup matches since [Festive Miracle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572790) activates at a **very good spot** with [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026). This acts as both a **speed boost and acceleration** at the same time, making her very hard to beat. The extra recovery basically makes her immune to Stamina debuffs, too.

We recommend running Christmas Oguri Cap as a **Pace Chaser**, where she is easiest to build with and the closest to catching up to Front Runners, with **Triple 7s**, [Corner Recovery](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537006), and [Hydrate](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536953) as her recoveries.

Since **Angling and Scheming** is at its best here, expect Front Runners to be tough competition for CM12. Among them, [Summer Maruzensky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/556411) and [Valentine's Mihono Bourbon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/580437) are the standout picks. [Seiun Sky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/547834) can also take advantage of her unique skill's full power; expect her to be one of the stronger Front Runners if well-built.

[Silence Suzuka](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536321) can be used here to prevent other Fronts from getting Angling and Scheming, but she will need to be trained under [Unity Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/545572) for [Runaway](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/563800). She can also potentially be used to make No Stopping Me usable for other Front Runners.

Late Surgers are likely the better backliners compared to End Closers, mostly because they need to compete for both **Let's Pump Some Iron** and **Moving Past, and Beyond** to have the best chance of winning. Their original users, [Mejiro Ryan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536297) and [Mejiro Dober](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572255), are both viable picks. Debuffers, especially those with [Dominator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922), can also work here since Medium opens the way to a wider pool of strong debuff skills.

[No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) users like [Mayano Top Gun](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536307) and [Kawakami Princess](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/568906) can also be good here because they don't have to rely on the [Yukino Bijin Wit SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537169) to obtain the skill.

・**Christmas Oguri Cap + 2 Late/End** - Oguri is the strongest ace here. Reduced chances of her being blocked while your 2 backliners can serve as backup aces. Backliners can also serve as debuffers with [Dominator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922).

・**2 Fronts + 1 Pace/Late** - Front-leaning composition. 2 Fronts guarantee support for each other. Non-Front is the backup ace.

・**1 Runaway + 2 Pace/Late** - Runaway for blocking Angling and Scheming. A counter strategy to Front-heavy builds.

・**3 Late Surgers** - Not as consistent, but potentially strong. Gamble for a chance of strong acceleration in the late-race (Let's Pump/Moving Past Beyond).

・**2 Aces + Debuffer** - Match-up dependent for stat/skill-checking weaker teams with a debuffer.

All compositions at least wants an **Ace**, which is the Uma you built to aim for 1st place. Additional aces can serve as a **backup** if your strongest fails. Dedicated **debuffers** can also be considered, but expect them to only serve to weaken opposing aces rather than win.

As the Aries Cup is on the shorter side of Medium races, it should be possible to win with **at least 600 Stamina**, provided you have enough Guts and Wit. However, consider aiming for **around 700 Stamina** (750 for Pace) to account for possible debuffers. You will also need to have **high Power** for the uphills and a decent amount of **Wit** to take advantage of the downhill.

[Oguri Cap (Ashen Miracle)](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572578) is one notable case where she's highly likely to still survive even with 600 Stamina if all of her recovery skills and unique skill [Festive Miracle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572790). You'll want to ensure she has really high Wit stat to guarantee activation of these skills.

The best scenario to train for the Aries Cup is the Trackblazer scenario. Trackblazer will provide **much higher stats** and **more skill points**, making it the preferred training environment. While you can still train in the [Unity Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/545572) to save on time, the statlines and skill selection likely won't be able to keep up with aces trained in the new scenario.

Just keep in mind that skills obtained from secret events won't be available with the Trackblazer scenario. This means if you want to train Runaway [Silence Suzuka](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536321), you'll need to train her in the Unity Cup.

Acceleration skills shorten the time it takes for your Umamusume to hit top speed. [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) is a must-have for **Front Runners** to help them secure their lead by the start of the late-race. Early acceleration like [Groundwork](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537044) and [Taking the Lead](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536941) should also help them get into a good position.

**Late Surgers** will want to have both [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) and [Moving Past, and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534) as both can activate right as late-race starts. This combo makes them very strong if your ace manages to trigger them. However, as they require 6th place at this point, this can be a gamble.

[No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) is also a great acceleration that can work for most styles since the late-race starts before the final corner. However, you will need to fit the [Yukino Bijin Wit SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537169) to get it if you're training in Trackblazer.

Speed skills, also known as velocity skills, increase the top speed of your Umamusume. They are a must not only to help your aces get in good positions where top speed is not yet unrestricted (such as in the mid-race), but to also reduce the negative effects of speed debuffs. Aim to get skills that activate during the **mid-race** ([Up-Tempo](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536970) / [Tail Held High](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537055) / [Ramp Up](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537007)) and ones that work on corners ([Corner Adept](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536954) / [Medium Corners](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537012)).

Recovery skills, besides **Triple 7s** for both [Christmas Oguri Cap](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572578) and [Summer Maruzensky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/556411), are completely optional since the Stamina requirement of the race is low enough. You can still choose to learn them to counter debuffers who use stamina reduction skills like [All-Seeing Eyes](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536906).

**Triple 7s** is notable for both Christmas Oguri Cap and Summer Maruzensky since the skill activation is positioned close before the late-race where their uniques skill effects can carry over.

[Oguri Cap (Ashen Miracle)](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572578) requires **3 recovery skills** to activate [Festive Miracle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572790). It's important to have **Triple 7s** since this will allow her unique to become a **strong acceleration skill**. She can also aim for around **500-600 Stamina** instead and focus more on **increasing Wit** to ensure her recoveries work.

[U=ma2](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543236) can also be inherited from [Agnes Tachyon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536293) since its activation should be very close to the Triple 7s location. Do note that this will only work if Oguri is in the 3rd or 4th positions on a corner, so it's best on her as a Pace Chaser.

[Maruzensky (Hot☆Summer Night)](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/556411) wants at least 1 recovery for the activation of [A Kiss for Courage](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/557607). **Triple 7s** is the best option for her unique skill since it activates at a good point in the mid-race (sometime before the late-race). Compared to Christmas Oguri Cap, you would still want to develop stamina normally since you'll only be using 1 recovery skill for this one compared to Oguri Cap's 3 required recovery skills.
