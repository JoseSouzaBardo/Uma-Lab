**Champions Meeting: Virgo Cup**
Event has ended.

Virgo Cup will run from **November 16, 2025** (UTC) until November 26, 2025. Like with previous Champions Meetings, the first few days will likely be for registration with the actual races happening a few days later.

Course Notes
・The revised version of the Hanshin 1,600m now has a **downhill** and **only one uphill** at the Homestretch of the Late-Race. This should make [Determined Descent](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536940) viable now. 

・**Final corner starts before the late-race**. This makes [Red Shift/LP1211-M](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883)virtually useless for Front and Pace but **viable for Late and End**. 

・**Final straight starts close to start of late-race**. [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) is usable again as it activates during the ramp-up and not too late, unlike in [Leo Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/554738). While it's not as strong as in [Cancer Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550078), it's still a usable acceleration skill for **End Closers**. 

・The 777m point for [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026) in near the midpoint of the race, just a few meters past the start of the final corner.

ⓘ Numbers with circles signify the order of the track part in the course. ① in Straights, for example, stands for the 1st straight.

Virgo Cup returns to a Mile track, similar to [Cancer Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550078). As such, several debuffers lose their effectiveness here, as Stamina is less important.

**Taiki Shuttle's** Unique skill, [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881), has been adjusted in the update to now trigger in the **second half of the final corner**. This gives her a good chance of activating it just as the late-race starts, making it one of the best acceleration skills for the race. However, it appears to be best used on Taiki Shuttle herself.

The biggest change to the dominance of Front Runners is the introduction of **Runaway** with [Silence Suzuka](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536321). This style lets her be even more aggressive than Front Runners, likely letting her secure the lead for most of the race. This should allow her to effectively **deny Angling and Scheming for other Front Runners**. However, it is also very stat-intensive and risky, as she will likely lose unless she has very high Stamina, Power, and Wit.

If you have Silence Suzuka, we only recommend running one as a Runaway to counter Front Runners or other Silence Suzukas but not as an ace.

[Runaway Style Guide](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/563800)

Even with the balance changes and the introduction of Runaway, Front Runners should still be good for Virgo Cup, mostly due to the power of [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060). However, the existence of Runaway Silence Suzuka could make activating it difficult.

If you still want to run them, [Seiun Sky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/547834) is the best choice, while [Summer Maruzensky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/556411) and [Smart Falcon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541253) (with enough Turf Sparks) are also good. As usual, try to run 2 Front Runners to increase the odds that one of your Aces can snag Angling and Scheming.

Also similar to Cancer Cup, several strong Late Surgers for Mile, like [Vodka](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536310) and [Grass Wonder](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536306) should still be usable. They are easier to build compared to Front Runners, but also **require some luck** to trigger their key skills at the right times.

End Closers like [Narita Taishin](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541215) and [Gold Ship](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536305) are also competitive, as [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) and [Encroaching Shadow](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541383) are usable again.

As a Mile race, the Virgo Cup is less stamina-intensive. It's best to maximize **Speed**, **Power**, and **Wit** for this race while aiming to meet the recommended Stamina and Guts to avoid slowing down near the end of the race.

For Runaways, **at least 700 Stamina** is recommended to last the whole track. It's also best to go **as high as you can for Power and Wit** to ensure you can block Front Runners and stay ahead.

For Front Runners, **Groundwork** and 3 early-race skills are a must to stay ahead early. 2 Navigational skills will also help (ex. [Dodging Danger](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537606) and [Prudent Positioning](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536944)). Note that Groundwork can be passed down as a White [Spark](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536822) from legacy parents.

Front Runners are also affected the most by [Late Starts](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/538274), so having the skill [Concentration](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536926)/[Focus](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536964) will come in handy. Note that this skill, however, is not always guaranteed. **Mid-race velocity skills** are also important to keep the lead and ensure [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) activates.

Late Surgers and End Closers will want **late-race acceleration** like [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), [Furious Feat](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536918), or [On Your Left](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536943) since these are very powerful when activated at the right time. [Red Shift](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883) can be considered, but its activation may not be at an ideal point. End Closers can also use [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039)/[Encroaching Shadow](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541383) again to make reaching their top speed in the final leg quicker.

In addition to the skills previously listed, we expect several skills to now be viable picks in the Virgo Cup. [Uma Stan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536961) is expected to be a good pick for any style that isn't a Front Runner. The **Burning Spirit skills** for Power and Guts are also decent choices from Unity Cup, if you don't have anything else to use your skill points on. Front Runners can also use [Early Lead](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536982)/[Taking the Lead](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536941) as a Groundwork trigger and to pick up more acceleration in the early-race.

[Straight Descent](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536993) and [Determined Descent](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536940) should be usable skills for Pace Chasers as there is now a downhill on the course. [Head-On](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564783) is a new acceleration skill found on Mayano Top Gun and Taiki Shuttle and looks to be a competitive acceleration skill for Pace Chasers, too. [Nimble Navigator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536987)/[No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) can be considered as well, as these can be strong when activated, but may be hard to trigger if the racers spread out too much in the late-race. [Corner Acceleration](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536985)/[Corner Connoisseur](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536936) may also be picked, but can be risky, considering they have a random activation point.

One notable change for recovery skills with the balance adjustments is the Speed Debuff attached to [Keen Eye](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536913) to all racers ahead of the user. This makes it worth considering for Late Surgers and End Closers in Virgo Cup.

Outside of that, Virgo Cup **does not need many recovery skills**, as it is a Mile race. One basic recovery skill should be enough if you need to lower the Stamina requirement or to **activate Summer Maruzensky's unique** skill, [A Kiss for Courage](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/557607). You can also learn recovery skills as a **safeguard against debuffers**. For most racers, they **can get away with not having a blue skill** if they have enough Stamina and Guts.

Aim for at least **34 Race Bonus** so you can get 4 of each stat instead of 3 when winning career races. If you have the event [Mejiro Dober SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/545935) or [Marvelous Sunday SR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536677) at max level, these are good ways to hit this threshold while being an extra Wit card. If you can't reach this, pick cards with **high or max Uncaps**, as they will provide better stats overall.
