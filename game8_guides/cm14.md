Course Notes
・1st uphill on a straight in the mid-race. 2nd uphill on the last straight, in the 1st half of the late-race. 

・Downhill occurs in the mid-race after the 1st uphill, covering the transition between the straight and corner. 

・Final corner starts in the mid-race and slightly ends sometime after the start of the late-race. Late-race corner accels such as [Budding Blossom](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594184) (Pace), [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) (Late), and [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) are good unique skill picks here. 

・Final straight is long, covering much of the late-race and is close to the start of it. Skills such as [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039), [Louder Tracen Cheer](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/596610), and [Victoria por plancha](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543206) should all be strong here.

Like the previous [Cancer Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550078), expect Front Runners to be a strong style thanks to [Seiun Sky's](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/547834) unique, [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060). The usual meta Front Runners, [Valentine Mihono Bourbon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/580437) and [Summer Maruzensky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/556411) are expected to be common aces. Front Runners will also want to inherit El Condor Pasa's [Victoria por plancha](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543206) to take advantage of the late-race final straight. If you're running triple Fronts, consider getting [Speed Eater](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537061) on all of them.

With the introduction of [Nishino Flower](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/590844) and her unique skill, [Budding Blossom](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594184), Pace Chasers now have a solid chance at winning more often. This will be the inherited skill of choice alongside [Give Mummy a Hug ♡](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/567567). [Taiki Shuttle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536317) herself can make some use of [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881), but it's too early when passing it to others.

Beware that Budding Blossom and Shooting for Victory! have positional requirements for **3rd to 4th**, so their effectiveness greatly depends on the styles your opponents bring. Positional requirements for Give Mummy a Hug ♡ are more relaxed, requiring just 2nd-4th place.

Both Late Surgers and End Closers have their own skills to help them win this race, but the most notable one is [Cheerleader King Halo's](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/596602) unique, [Louder! Tracen Cheer!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/596610). Due to this unique's position requirement for the first half of the race, this may be more reliable on **End Closers**. Late Surgers will also want to try gambling for 6th with [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), while End Closers should be able to take advantage of [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039)/[Encroaching Shadow](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541383).

・**2 Fronts + 1 Any** - Front-leaning composition. 2 Fronts guarantee support for each other. 3rd ace is a backup, but can be another Front to push down Pace Chasers.

・**2 Pace + 1 Any** - Pace-focused line-up. 2 Pace makes it easier to meet the challenge requirement. May be difficult to use Budding Blossom if there are no Fronts or too many Fronts due to requiring 3rd-4th.

・**3 Late/Ends** - Focuses on maximizing hits with [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) and [Louder! Tracen Cheer!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/596610) with backliners, taking advantage of their acceleration and top speed output in the late-race. **End Closers are preferred** when possible.

・**2 Aces + Debuffer** - Match-up dependent for stat/skill-checking weaker teams with a debuffer.

More acceleration skills have been introduced since the last Mile CM, with [Budding Blossom](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594184) being the accel of choice for **Pace** (3rd-4th) and [Louder! Tracen Cheer!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/596610) for **Late/End**. Accels that work on at the start of the late-race corner, such as [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) (1st) and [Let's Pump Some Iron!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) (Late/End, 6th) will continue to be used here as the strongest options.

Due to the position of the **final straight**, using skills that activate as soon as they reach it should also be usable as **secondary acceleration**. These include [Victoria por plancha ☆](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543206) (Front), [Give Mummy a Hug ♡](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/567567) (Pace), and [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) (End).

Random acceleration like [Head-On](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564783), [Slick Surge](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536973), and [Updrafters](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536974), and their upgrades can also be used if you don't have other options, but these are lower priority if the skills above are available.

Mid-race velocity skills are still important to maintain good positions before entering the late-race. General ones like [Ramp Up](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537007), [Playtime's Over](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537064), and [Tail Held High](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537055) are all good options. The **distance and style corner and straight skills** should also be good value if you can pick them up.

[Uma Stan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536961) is a recommended pick, as usual, for all styles that aren't Front, so they can start in good positions early on. **End Closers** will specifically want to get [Daring Strike](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/577265) when possible.

Due to [Taiki Shuttle Speed SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/603338) being available starting Round 1 of the Gemini Cup, [Mile Maven](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536937) should be available on all characters by then. This skill is recommended for Fronts and Pace if you're willing to borrow this card and rebuild for Round 2 and the Finals.

Stamina debuffs (Flustered, Subdued) are low priority since it should be fairly easy to hit the required Stamina.
