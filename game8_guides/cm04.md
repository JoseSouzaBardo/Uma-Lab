Course Notes
・There are **2 uphills** in the race, 1st uphill at the Early-Race of ①Straight and the 2nd uphill is in the Homestretch of the Late-Race. 

 ┗ This course technically loops around, so the 2nd uphill is the same as the 1st. 

・There are no technical downhills, making certain skills like [Go-Home Specialist](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536925) inactive for the duration of the race. 

・The **second half** (middle) of the race is on the ②Straight. 

・The 777m point for [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026) in near the end of the Mid-Race on ③Corner.

ⓘ Numbers with circles signify the order of the track part in the course. ① in Straights, for example, stands for the 1st straight.

Note that the Unity Cup scenario will arrive on **November 6, 2025**, which will be in the middle of the Leo Cup schedule of Round 2. The new scenario will **give better training results**, so you may expect to make some emergency training for at least Round 2 of the Champions Meeting.

We recommend you still go with the usual setup focusing on Speed and Stamina first, then others. But if you do train during the Unity Cup scenario, take advantage of the scenario cards already like the **SSR/R Riko Kashimoto Pal** and **SSR Rice Shower Power** cards.

For Leo Cup, we are expecting to see multiple debuffers, especially [Nice Nature](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536287). For most players, an **Ace with 2 Debuffers** or **2 Aces with 1 Debuffer** are viable setups to run. **End Closers**, like [Gold Ship](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536305), can be used to **avoid some of the debuffs**. If you can get enough End sparks, consider having your Ace as an End Closer since the Debuffers are typically run with a Late Surger style.

If you have [Summer Maruzensky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/559645), you may want to pair her with another Front Runner like [Seiun Sky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/547834), so you'll have a better chance of securing [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) for one of them.

You can also run a Hybrid setup, where your Late Surger/End Closer will try to win while carrying some debuffs. However, you must be more selective with the debuffs, as you'll also want to have skills to actually help them win.

[Wedding Mayano Top Gun](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541251) is one example of a Hybrid Ace that is a surprise top entry into this Champions Meeting, due to her being capable of becoming an [End Closer](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/544879) with [Mystifying Murmur](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536891). It also helps that she get a guarantee of [Swinging Maestro](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536895) at Potential Level 5.

All styles appreciate **as many velocity-boosting skills as possible** to minimize the reduction from [Dominator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922).

For Front Runners, **Groundwork and three green skills** is a must to stay ahead early. Note that Groundwork can be passed down as a White [Spark](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536822) from legacy parents. They are also affected the most by [Late Starts](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/538274), so having the skill [Concentration](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536926)/[Focus](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536964) will come in handy. While you can substitute a green skill for Focus, it's not guaranteed to activate and can make Groundwork useless.

Late Surgers and End Closers will want **late-game acceleration** like Red Shift, Shooting for Victory, or even Let's Pump Some Iron to catch up in the final part of the race as well. Note that for End Closers, [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039)does not appear to give any noticeable effect despite being able to activate.
