**Similar Races**: **Tenno Sho (Spring) (Hanshin)**
Course Notes
・Late-race starts on a straight. [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) will be the acceleration of choice for End Closers. Front Runners will want to run **Kitasan Black's unique**, [Victory Cheer!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/586148). 

・The first late-race corner starts a bit late. This means skills like [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) are not as effective. Do not learn final corner accelerations like [Red Shift](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883) or [Shooting for Victory](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881). 

・**2 Downhills** and **2 Uphills** mean investment in **Wit and Power** should help. None are particularly good for acceleration, but recoveries ([Restless](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536911) and [Go-Home Specialist](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536925)) could be used. 

・Heavy conditions will require more Stamina and Power stats.

The Pisces Cup is a very long race measuring at 3,200m. To make sure your aces can survive and maximize their performance at the end, you'll ideally want around **1100 Stamina with 2 Gold Recoveries** to be safe. If you can't hit this goal, it should be possible to win with **at least 1000 Stamina**, but with the risk of not being at their best during the late-race.

Aim for as much Wit as you can as well, especially for the Front Runners, so they can maintain good positions and reliably activate skills throughout the race.

![Image 119: Trackblazer Logo](https://img.game8.co/4416691/3cd8aae7760a424289dfc655c859c430.jpeg/show)
The best scenario to train for the Pisces Cup is the Trackblazer scenario. Trackblazer will provide **much higher stats** and **more skill points**, making it the preferred way to train. While you can still train in the [Unity Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/545572) to save on time, the statlines and skill selection likely won't be able to keep up with aces trained in the new scenario.

Just keep in mind that skills obtained from secret events won't be available with the Trackblazer scenario. This means if you want to train Runaway [Silence Suzuka](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536321), you'll need to train her in the Unity Cup. [Mayano Top Gun](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536307) must get [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) from sparks or rival races since she doesn't get her event here.

Front Runners will want to inherit **Kitasan Black's unique skill** to gain an acceleration skill for the race. Likewise, End Closers will want to learn [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) to increase their chances of victory. Other styles will have to rely on random late-race acceleration like [Head-On](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564783) or [On Your Left](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536943), which isn't ideal. [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) can be considered for a strong acceleration skill, but it is not easily obtainable in Trackblazer anymore, requiring use of the [Yukino Bijin Wit SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537169). It's a viable pick for a Wit card as long as you hit at least 50 Race Bonus for your deck.

Mid-race velocity skills are also highly recommended for the Pisces Cup. If your deck can spare enough Race Bonus to fit in [Kitasan Black SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537147), get [Professor of Curvature](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536923) from it. End Closers can likewise try to fit in [Daring Strike](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/577265) from the [Admire Vega SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/575513) if possible. Other white speed skills such as **Corner and Straightaway** skills, [Ramp Up](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537007), [Uma Stan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536961), and [Tail Held High](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537055) should also be good picks in general.

The [Team Sirius SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/587507) also provides a great rare skill ([Best in Japan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/590049)) that **increases velocity on the final corner**. This **works for all styles**, although it can be expensive to learn.

The Trackblazer scenario-specific skill, [Radiant Star](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/586339), is a possible option to learn since it provides speed, acceleration, and recovery all at once. However, it's expensive and activates randomly in the second half of the race. We only recommend taking it if you have spare skill points and are willing to gamble on this skill.

For the Pisces Cup support cards, aim for decks with **at least 2 Speed Cards** and **ideally 2 Stamina Cards** (one of which can be substituted with Riko Kashimoto SSR). [Super Creek SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536698) will almost always be needed as it has [Swinging Maestro](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536895) and 10% Race Bonus at max level. If your trainee has a 20% or better Stamina growth, it's possible to substitute the Stamina card with another card. The remaining cards are best filled with **Wit cards**, but you can replace them with another Speed card, a high race bonus card, or a card with a good rare skill.

Under the Trackblazer scenario, aim to get at least [50 Race Bonus](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572831) to gain more stats and skill points from winning races. If this isn't possible, try to get as close as possible with high-level cards.
