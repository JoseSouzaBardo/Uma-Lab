* * *

 ・The late-race begins near the exit of the final corner. 

・Remaining 777m (ex. for [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026)) is situated some lengths after the middle of the race (800m). 

・First uphill, followed by a downhill, is in the mid-race, right after the early-race and before the first corner. 

・Second uphill is on the last straight (homestretch) afer the last corner in the late-race.

ⓘ Numbers with circles signify the order of the track part in the course. ① in Straights, for example, stands for the 1st straight.

For the Cancer Cup, aim to maximize **Speed** and **Power** this time as much as you can while just having enough **Stamina** to survive the distance. **Wit** is also essential, since it will improve your positioning as well as the chances for your skills to activate. **Guts** can follow last, since this acts like your Umamusume's extended stamina for the Last Spurt of the racecourse.
