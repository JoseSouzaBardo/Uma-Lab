Course Notes
・1 large downhill is only present in the early-race. 1 uphill is located around the middle of the final straight in the late-race. 

・The section at the beginning of the race before the corner is considered neither a corner nor a straight, so all **straight skills will only happen on the final straight** in the late-race. An exception is [Smart Falcon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541253)'s [SPARKLY ☆ STARDOM](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543188), which will still activate in the mid-race before the corner (even if the in-game text specifies it needing a straight). 

・Late-race starts around midway through the final corner, so most strong final corner skills are still good here, notably [Shooting for Victory](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881).

The Leo Cup is expected to be dominated by Pace Chasers, particularly because they have access to two powerful accelerations in [Budding Blossom](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594184) and [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881) Just beware that they require 3rd to 4th positions to activate. Among them, their original users [Nishino Flower](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/590844) and [regular Taiki Shuttle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536317) are expected to be top contenders as they can use these at full power. Other strong Sprinters like both versions of [Curren Chan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537177) and [Hishi Akebono](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564091) also tend to be Pace Chasers, so expect a lot of them for this race.

Players without these other options should run [Sakura Bakushin O](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536298) as a Pace Chaser and try to grab both accelerations.

Front Runners can use [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) as usual due to the late-race starting on a corner. However, they serve another purpose, which is to **manipulate the position of Pace Chasers**. Budding Blossom and Shooting for Victory can actually be used if you're running 3 Front Runners due to being available at 3rd or 4th. Triple Fronts also makes those accels stricter for Pace, as they'll likely need to fight for 4th to activate them.

Among the Front Runners, [Valentine Mihono Bourbon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/580437) and [Summer Maruzensky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/556411) will be strong and won't require as many sparks as the rest. [Smart Falcon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541253) is also a strong pick, provided she gets enough Turf sparks. [Silence Suzuka](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536321) is also a decent option, but it's best to use her as a regular Front.

Late Surgers may not be as strong as Front Runners and Pace Chasers but they have a fighting chance with the recent change to [On Your Left!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536943) and the addition of [Leap Forward](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/608322) after the [July 1 Balance Adjustments](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/538351).

You can try to inherit [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), but lobbies will likely be crowded with Pace Chasers and Front Runners, making it difficult to climb up into 6th place. Even [Louder! Tracen Cheer!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/596610)activates much later, so they'll likely be left behind.

End Closers also benefit from having [Leap Forward](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/608322) as one of their main acceleration skills in short distance tracks but that is the only reliable acceleration they have. [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) can be very powerful but is often unreliable on the final corner.

The biggest wildcard for the Leo Cup is the introduction of [Summer Gold Ship](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/607649). Her unique skill will allow debuffers to use their rare skills outside their intended tracks. Among these, [Dominator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922) and [Battle Formation](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/570845) are expected to be mainly used. Just note that a double debuffer strategy is risky since you are only relying on a single ace to win. Overall, it's not recommended to bring them due to the highly luck-based late-race, but expect that some may appear.

・**2 Fronts + 1 Any** - Front-leaning composition. 2 Fronts guarantee support for each other. The 3rd ace can be another Front to push down Pace or a Pace Chaser to compete against the others. Runaway is not recommended.

・**2 Pace + 1 Any** - Pace-leaning composition. 2 Pace makes it easier to meet the challenged condition for Budding Blossom. 3 Pace can be brought to account for lobbies with no Front Runners.

・**2 Ace + 1 Debuffer** - Somewhat riskier strategy that makes use of a Speed or Accel debuffer to weaken opponents.

As a Sprint race, Stamina is of least concern. Instead, try to focus on **capping Speed and Power** to maximize their top speed and acceleration in the late-race. Having Power above 1200 in particular gives your trainee a shot in using the [Fully Charged](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/607687) mechanic, which is additional acceleration at the start of late-race.

If capping Power is difficult, try to aim for at least 1000. If you're able to, raise Wit and Guts as high as possible. Getting **Turf S** is also more important than Sprint S mostly due to the importance of Power and acceleration in Sprints.

**Legacy accelerations** are **still a must-have** for all styles. Fronts must take [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060), Pace must learn both [Budding Blossom](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594184) and [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881) while Late/End need [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235). It's **possible to play around with these**, like giving A&S to a Pace if you're expecting lobbies without a Front or giving the Pace accels to a triple Front team, since these can also be valid.

Following the [July 1, 2026 balance adjustments](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/607082), **Pace Chasers** can consider getting either [Head-On](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564783) or [Neck and Neck](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594185), as they were adjusted to trigger earlier in the late-race. Likewise, **Late Surgers** should try to fit [Slick Surge](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536973) or [On Your Left!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536943) for the same reason. [Unrestrained](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536927) was not adjusted for Fronts and should only be taken as a gamble. **All styles** can now use [Sprinting Gear](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537016)/[Turbo Sprint](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536899) as well, as it is no longer limited to a straight. A new acceleration skill, **Leap Forward**, should also be considered for Late and End since this can easily be triggered basically at the start of late-race. This can be obtained through Sparks or as a random skill hint in Trackblazer.

Pace Chasers also have the unique advantage of making the most of the early downhill in the race. They can take [Straight Descent](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536993) although they can also use Groundwork for early acceleration to get ahead. This is best paired with a velocity skill like [Downhill Speedster](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/605127) or [Uma Stan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536961).

Due to the strong importance of hitting the 3rd and 4th positions before the late-race for this race, it's important to learn enough mid-race velocity skills to maintain these. [In High Spirits](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/580961) from the [Bakushin Guts SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/580436) is notable for activating at a good time and possibly letting the speed boost apply during acceleration. **Corner velocity skills** will also be must-haves when you have them.

For the Unity Cup decks, we recommend using **Speed-Wit decks** to maximize training time and getting the most out of Spirit Bursts. Generally, 3 Speed and 3 Wit should be fine for most, but you can adjust these to 2 Speed with high growth trainees (15%+) or add in a Guts card with strong skills such as Sakura Bakushin O Guts for Sprint skills and [In High Spirits](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/580961). The Throne Group SSR is optional since it doesn't have Sprint skills, but it can be used to replace a Wit or Speed card for stats and skill points. Aim for more Power sparks or use cards with Power Bonus if you're struggling to hit the Power cap.

You may also try Guts decks if you want a higher stat total, but these can be inconsistent and may be lacking in important skills.

Although a 3 Guts and 1 Speed deck has the potential for higher stats, it's harder to use, so a 2 Guts and 2 Speed deck should be fine. If you're still lacking in Power, you can replace Guts cards with Power support cards, but it's recommended to get **more Power sparks instead** or use Speed or Guts cards with **Power Bonus**.
