The Capricorn Cup has been officially announced to run starting from February 13, 2026 at 10:00PM UTC until February 19, 2026. This is the **first Sprint Champions Meeting** and will be patterned after the [Takamatsunomiya Kinen](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/539045).

Course Notes
・Entire late-race is on a straight. Late-race skills that work on corners like [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235)will not activate. [Moving Past, and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534) and [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881) will likely activate too early to be useful. **Straight acceleration skills** should be very good for this track. [Red Shift/LP1211-M](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883) can be usable for Late/End styles. 

・Track has a **very long downhill**. Theoretically, [Straight Descent](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536993) can be powerful if it activates right as late-race starts, but the length of the downhill makes it too random and unreliable. **High Wit** is necessary to be competitive. 

・Track has an **uphill immediately after the downhill**. [Highlander](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536994) can be usable, but it activates late. 

・[Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026) will activate at 423m, which is in the first corner of mid-race.

The Capricorn Cup is the first Sprint Champions Meeting. As of January 2026, the options for Sprint-focused runners are currently very limited. Of these, [Sakura Bakushin O](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536298) is the most accessible runner and will likely appear in every race. [Curren Chan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537177) and [Hishi Akebono](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564091) are also Sprint specialists and should be competitive for this race. [King Halo](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536295) is another F2P option, but she can be difficult to use due to a lack of good innate Sprint skills and an inconsistent unique skill.

If you want to use other aces for the Capricorn Cup, you'll need to provide **Sprint sparks** for them to reach at least A aptitude. [Smart Falcon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541253) can be a strong runner for the race, too, but will need **10 Turf sparks** as well. Make sure to check your Umamusume's aptitudes to know how many sparks you'll need to make them viable.

Take note that you can only raise aptitude by **4 levels with sparks**, with the rest being gained through Inspiration. It's best to avoid training an ace with a G or F in Sprint due to the difficulty and randomness involved.

Compared to previous races, **Turf S** is more recommended than Distance S since you want more acceleration in this race. Acceleration skills are also somewhat harder to come by and use effectively here. **Distance S** is still a good choice, if you can't get lucky enough to hit Turf S.

Since Stamina is less of an issue with Sprint races, it's best to focus on **maximizing Speed and Wit** for this race. The Soft conditions will also require high enough **Power** to stay competitive.

For the Capricorn Cup, acceleration skill options are somewhat limited. End Closers can reliably use [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039), but others may have to use skills with random activation. All styles can use [Rushing Gale!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536909) or [Turbo Sprint](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536899), but with the risk of activating too early or too late. Still, they're good to pick up since there aren't lot of other acceleration options. Late Surgers can pick up [On Your Left!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536943) or [Red Shift](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883) from Maruzensky, and Pace-Chasers can use [Head-On](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564783) or [Give Mummy a Hug](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/567567) from Halloween Super Creek. [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) is somewhat inconsistent here, considering the late-race starts after the final corner, but it's still the strongest option available if you're willing to gamble on it.

Front Runners cannot use Angling and Scheming on this race, so they'll need to pull away early with [Groundwork](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537044) and [Taking the Lead](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536941). [Victoria por plancha](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543206) is the late acceleration skill of choice for Front Runners due to easily satisfying the conditions and triggering right as they hit the final straight. They can also use No Stopping Me! if you're running one alongside Runaway Silence Suzuka.

As usual, take any mid-race velocity skills you can afford for the rest of the skills, leaning more toward corner skills.

Above are sample deck setups you can try for training your Umamusume for the Capricorn Cup. Remember to swap cards if they conflict with your trainee, and note that the randomized nature of Career runs means you'll have to make multiple runs until you get a desirable career result.

Since this is a Sprint race, you'll want to maximize both **Speed** and **Wit** by having **at least 2 of each** card in your deck. You also opt to add a strong Guts card like [Haru Urara (Bestest Prize)](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536294) or a Pal card like [Tazuna Hayakawa](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536694) or [Riko Kashimoto](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/561497) (or both) to make runs go more smoothly.

Also aim to hit **at least 34 [race bonus](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572831)**, as this maximizes stat gains during career races. If you can't hit this, try to use **high or max uncapped cards** instead, as these will provide better stats overall.
