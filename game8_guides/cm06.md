**Champions Meeting: Libra Cup**
Event has ended.

Libra Cup will run from December 8 to 18, 2025. Like with previous Champions Meetings, the first few days will likely be for registration, with the actual races happening a few days later.

**Similar Races**: [Kikuka Sho](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/539031)
Course Notes
・Kyoto 3,000m starts on an uphill. Another uphill is present near late-race. [Highlander](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536994) can be useful as an early acceleration or late-race accel, depending on where it activates. 

・**Late-race starts on a straight**. [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) is looking to be one of the best skills for the race, making **End Closers** powerful if it activates. 

・**Final corner is deep in late-race**. This makes [Red Shift/LP1211-M](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883) and [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881)unusable for all styles since they will be at max speed at this point. [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) are still usable since they check for any late-race corner, but they're not as powerful compared to other tracks.

Libra Cup is a Long Race, so Stamina is the most important stat to consider if you want your aces to win. Runners with an innate recovery skill should have a slight advantage, as it frees up some Support Cards for them.

[Narita Taishin](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541215) is currently the only way to access [Encroaching Shadow](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541383), the upgraded version of [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039). This skill alone makes **End Closers**, like [Gold Ship](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536305), very competitive for the Libra Cup, and is a must-have if you're building them. However, End Closers (and Late Surgers) can still be a bit of a gamble, so bringing other skills like [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) should increase their chances of victory.

**Manhattan Cafe** should be arriving just before Libra Cup starts and can be another viable End Closer with the right sparks.

Front Runners will likely struggle because the final leg of the race is fairly long. This means there's a greater chance they will be overtaken by the faster Late and End styles.

In addition to that, [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) won't be as impactful due to the late-race corner being slightly late in activation. [Seiun Sky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/547834) herself can still make the most of it, but others who inherit it might struggle to keep the lead on the final straight. Runaway Silence Suzuka is also not viable at all due to the extreme stamina requirements for this style.

Due to the Stamina requirements of the race, debuffers can be brought to cripple rival aces that aren't prepared. However, note that Mystifying Murmur will not work, as this is a Long race.

As a Long race, **Stamina is the most important stat** to ensure your runners don't slow down during the late-race. Aim for at least 900 Stamina to survive, but **pushing it to at least 1000 Stamina is best** to improve your chances of winning. You'll also need sufficient Power to overcome the two uphills in the race. If you're struggling to hit the Power requirement, aim for **at least 600 Power**, but still try to increase it as much as you can. For Wit, at least 600 is ideal, but having **400 Wit** should be enough to activate skills reliably.

Generally, try to **learn 2 rare recovery skills** to be able to satisfy the Stamina requirement for the race. More than 900 Stamina should be the goal for most styles to survive with two gold recoveries, but having **higher Stamina would be better** to account for Rushed, potential debuffers, or failed skill activations. If you're able to reach this requirement and get high enough Speed, you can push stamina higher to ensure your aces can make **maximize their performance in the last spurt**, potentially allowing them to overtake those with less stamina.

The [Rice Shower Power SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/561494) is a good Support Card to bring, as it can give both [Swinging Maestro](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536895) and [Cooldown](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536939) when training in Unity Cup.

If you can only fit **one rare recovery skill**, try to aim for a **combined Stamina and Guts total of at least 1600**, with Stamina always above 1000. This is riskier, but it can work out, especially if you know you will be getting a lot of Stamina and Guts with the Riko Support Card.

For all styles, acceleration skills that activate as soon as the late-race begins are good. [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039)/[Encroaching Shadow](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541383) will hit this often for End Closers, making it a must if you're running this style. [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) is also worth considering for all styles (even Fronts that aren't in the lead), but it will prevent you from getting Cooldown if you're getting it through [Unity Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/545572). Both [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) are still good picks, but activate a bit later than ideal.

Mid and late-race velocity skills are also good to have on every runner. Late skills are particularly good as the late-race is fairly long here. Having a velocity boost can help them overtake at a crucial moment.

As usual for Front Runners, strong early-race acceleration, such as **[Groundwork](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537044) and three green skills** or [Early Lead](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536982) are a must to secure the lead immediately. Having the skill [Concentration](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536926)/[Focus](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536964) is also good to reduce the effects of Late Starts.

Recovery skills are a must if you're to survive the entire length of the race without slowing down. Remember to pick **Blue Bloom** in Unity Cup if you have the support card equipped to get [Cooldown](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536939).

If you want to get [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) from Unity Cup instead, you can learn the other gold recoveries listed above, depending on their style.

Above are sample deck setups you can try for training your Umamusume for Libra Cup. Remember to swap cards if they conflict with your trainee, and note that the randomized nature of Career runs means you'll have to make multiple runs until you get a desirable career result.

For the Stamina decks, you can replace one of the SSRs if you're able to get a gold recovery skill from your trainee's innate skills. For the Power decks, [Rice Shower SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/561494) is almost mandatory, as it's the main source of rare recovery skills. [Riko Kashimoto](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/561497) (both SSR and R) is also a powerful card that can essentially replace a Stamina card when used in [Unity Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/545572).

Also aim to hit **at least 34 race bonus**, as this maximizes stat gains during career races. If you can't hit this, try to use **high or max uncapped cards** instead, as these will provide better stats overall.
