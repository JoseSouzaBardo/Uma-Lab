The Gemini Cup's race is a **Turf Long** (3200m) racecourse that accommodates the [Right-handed](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537059). Its distance is also a multiple of 400m, so it is compatible with [Standard Distance](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537011). For a similar race you can emulate as the Gemini Cup, the closest is the [Tenno Sho (Spring)](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/539041).

The Gemini Cup will be a big trial by fire for PvP training: maxing out [Stamina](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/535990) while trying to balance [Speed](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/535937). You will need to **build both Speed and Stamina above 1000**. Unless your are extremely lucky, expect most of the time to have your Power under 700 as the result of maximizing only Speed and Stamina while contributing to Wit.

The values above represent their effectiveness in a general sense. Different recovery skill effects and behavior will vary.

When aiming for maximum Speed and Power while having high Wit, it's expected that building high Stamina (especially more than 1000) in this scenario is a tall order.

This why it is recommended to **supplement Stamina with Recovery Skills** since they serve as efficient options to have if you cannot build enough stamina. Having them will also help **pad your stamina against debuff skills**.

[Pace Chasers](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/544592) can still be contenders if built well (recommended with Gold Recovery skills), while getting [Front Runners](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/544485) to sustain a lead will be a tall order due to the difficulties of obtaining the necessary skills and stamina requirements.

Because of this, it is a viable strat to **convert Umamusume to End Closer styles** for easier stamina management and a higher potential for overtakes in the last spurt of the race.
