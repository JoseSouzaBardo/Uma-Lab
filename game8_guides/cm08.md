**Champions Meeting: Sagittarius Cup**

**January 19 - 29, 2026 (UTC)**
Event has ended.

The Sagittarius Cup will start on **January 19, 2026 (UTC)** and run until January 29, 2026 (UTC). Like with previous Champions Meetings, the first few days will likely be for registration, with the actual races happening a few days later.

Course Notes
・Nakayama has 3 uphills and 1 downhill. None of them is useful for acceleration, so avoid[Highlander](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536994) and [Straight Descent](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536993). However, they can be used for recovery like [Restless](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536911) and [Go-Home Specialist](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536925). 

・**Late-race starts on a straight**. [Encroaching Shadow](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541383)/[Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039) are some of the best skills for the race, making **End Closers** a strong style. It also makes [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) more reliable since this is before they spread out. 

・First late-race corner is close enough to the start of late-race. [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) are still **usable**, but they're not as powerful compared to other tracks. [Moving Past, and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534) will have the same conditions as Let's Pump Some Iron and **can be used together** when inherited. 

・**Final corner is deep in late-race**. This makes [Red Shift/LP1211-M](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883) and [Shooting for Victory!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536881)unusable for all styles since they will be at max speed at this point.

As a Long race, **Stamina is the most important stat** to ensure your runners don't slow down during the late-race. Aim for at least 900 Stamina to survive, but **pushing it to around 1000 Stamina is best** to improve your chances of winning. You'll also need sufficient Power to overcome the three uphills in the race. If you're struggling to hit the Power requirement, aim for **at least 600 Power**, but still try to increase it as much as you can. For Wit, at least 600 is ideal, but having **400 Wit** should be enough to activate skills reliably.

For all styles, acceleration skills that activate as soon as the late-race begins are good. [Straightaway Spurt](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537039)/[Encroaching Shadow](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541383) will hit this often for End Closers, making it a must if you're running this style. [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) is another strong skill that you can get easily in the [Unity Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/545572) by **choosing Team Carrots** as your team name. Although this will prevent you from getting [Cooldown](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536939) from the Scenario, the impact of No Stopping Me! on races more than makes up for it.

Both [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060) for Front Runners and [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) for Late Surgers and End Closers are still good picks, but they will not be as powerful compared to other tracks. [Moving Past, and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534) can be paired with [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235) as well to gain a lot of acceleration in the late-race.

Due to the conditions of Christmas Oguri Cap's unique skill, we highly recommend going with **3 white recoveries** for her rather than 1 Gold recovery. She can **lower her Stamina requirement to around 750**, but we suggest aiming for slightly above 900, if possible. What's important is that **Triple 7s activates last**, as it's in a good position for the race. Try to get **as much Wit as possible** to ensure the recoveries activate. You can try a deck with at least 2 Wit cards since her Stamina requirement is more relaxed.

Above are sample deck setups you can try for training your Umamusume for the Sagittarius Cup. Remember to swap cards if they conflict with your trainee, and note that the randomized nature of Career runs means you'll have to make multiple runs until you get a desirable career result.

For the Stamina decks, you can replace one of the SSRs if you're able to get a gold recovery skill from your trainee's innate skills. For the Power decks, [Rice Shower SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/561494) is almost mandatory, as it's the main source of rare recovery skills. [Riko Kashimoto](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/561497) (both SSR and R) is also a powerful card that can essentially replace a Stamina card when used in [Unity Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/545572).

Also aim to hit **at least 34 [race bonus](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572831)**, as this maximizes stat gains during career races. If you can't hit this, try to use **high or max uncapped cards** instead, as these will provide better stats overall.
