Course Notes
ⓘ The course display is taken from the Japanese version of the game. Translations were made and additional markers were added for convenience. 

* * *

・This track has no uphills or downhills. Therefore, stamina requirements are going to be fairly steep for a 2000m race. 

・The corners of the track are fairly short, but they're not classified as sharp turns. The **late-race starts on the third corner**, so final corner accels aren’t worth taking. 

・Good accelerations will be the usual corner legacies ([Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060), [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), and [Moving, Past and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534)). The skill [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) is still a strong, flexible skill and is usable by other styles. 

・As a Medium track, [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026) is in a prime spot for Christmas Oguri’s [Festive Miracle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572790). [U=ma2](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543236) is also a great activator.

As another 2000m Medium race, we're expecting Christmas Oguri Cap to be the dominant ace again, much like the [Aries Cup (CM12)](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/587444). Just like that race, she has 2 good activators for [Festive Miracle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572790) in [Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026) and [U=ma2](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543236), making it unlikely for her to fail to use her powerful unique. In addition, her unique also being a recovery makes her not have to worry about stamina debuffers, making her the most effective runner by far.

Copano Rickey is very much hyped as a meta Dirt ace, and she can certainly prove her worth here. Although Christmas Oguri Cap is more consistent, Copano Rickey should be able to score some wins against her, provided her **unique skill** triggers as **close to the start of the late-race** as possible. You can even run both of them together to solidify your chances of winning, as they're both very strong contenders. Just make sure to give her **6 green skills** for the race to maximize her unique's effect.

Outside of these top two, **Front Runners** should be able to compete thanks to being able to use [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060). **Both versions of Smart Falcon** are great picks as they're specifically built for Dirt tracks.

**Late Surgers** also have **lots of acceleration skills** to choose from thanks to the [July adjustments](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/538351) done to [Slick Surge](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536973) and [Take the Chance](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/591704). Some of the best runners for this style are [Inari One](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/597849) and [Agnes Digital](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/566109).

As a Medium race with Christmas Oguri Cap, double [Dominators](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922) are expected yet again. This time, they can also run [564 Escapades](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/607867) to also carry a **second rare debuff** like [Keen Eye](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536913).

The high stamina requirement of this race also means **Stamina debuffers** with [Mystifying Murmur](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536891) could see some use, but they likely won't be able to affect Christmas Oguri Cap. To counter them, we recommend taking one rare recovery.

・**2 Fronts + 1 Any** - Front-leaning composition. 2 Fronts guarantee support for each other. 3rd ace can be another Front or a Pace like Christmas Oguri Cap.

・**1 Ace + 2 Debuffer** - Team composition for Christmas Oguri Cap. Not recommended for most other aces. [Dominator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922) with [Keen Eye](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536913) is the recommended debuff combo.

・**3 Lates/Ends** - Gambling-focused strategy, must be able to catch up to Christmas Oguri or Copano Rickey.

Following the [July balance adjustments](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/538351), several acceleration skills, including [Head-On](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564783), [Slick Surge](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536973), and [Take the Chance](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/591704), have been buffed to be very usable as they are more likely to activate closer to the beginning of late-race.

The usual **legacy accelerations** for Medium ([Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060), [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), and [Moving Past, and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534)) are still **must-haves** as the late-race starts on a corner. [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) is also a strong acceleration for all styles as the final corner is positioned in the late-race.

Also take note to avoid learning too many late-race accelerations. Generally, you'll want to max out at 3 skills, including the inherited skills, and limit it to 1 rare skill. **Front Runners** should also try to gain as much **early acceleration** as they can with [Taking the Lead](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536941) and [Groundwork](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537044).

Important speed skills include [Uma Stan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536961) for all styles that aren't Front, skills with a chance to last until the late-race starts like [Sunny Sign](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/608663), and skills that activate on **corners** (avoid Final Corner skills).

Late-race velocity like [Come What May](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/570231) and [Triumphant Pulse](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536882) are also good picks if you have spare skill points for securing the win.

With the introduction of [564 Escapades](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/607867), debuffers can be built with **two rare debuffs**. Generally, they'll want to take [Keen Eye](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536913) from [Gold City Speed SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536700) and hope 564 Escapades activates Keen Eye so they can fire off 2 rare speed debuffs.

Stamina debuffs can potentially be viable, but they likely won't be as effective against Christmas Oguri Cap, as she's likely built with lots of recovery skills. If you do want to run stamina debuffers, we recommend learning only [Mystifying Murmur](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536891), as it has a normally hard condition that can be bypassed entirely by **564 Escapades**. If you're willing to take risks, you can learn the other rare debuffs, although beware of their drawbacks. [All-Seeing Eyes](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536906) is limited by FOV, so may miss against Fronts. [Stamina Siphon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/570230) is fairly weak for a rare, being closer to a Subdued or Flustered skill in strength.

Just keep in mind that the debuffers are unlikely to win, so you'll only need to **focus on building their Wit** to ensure the skills activate.
