Course Notes
・The race has 2 downhills: one at the start of the race and a long one that extends from mid-race to late-race. High Wit is recommended to make the most of these. [Camping Mejiro Dober's](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/603279) unique should be viable here. 

・Uphills follow both of the downhills. High Power is also recommended to overcome these. 

・Late-race starts on the corner before the final corner. The final straight is very far from the start of the late-race, so it's best to invest mostly in corner skills here. 

・[Triple 7s](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537026) is situated at a good point where having both velocity and accel are very effective, making [Christmas Oguri](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572578) a significantly strong pick.

Christmas Oguri Cap is once again expected to dominate a Champions Meeting as the Triple 7s activation happens very close to the start of the late-race. Just like in the [Aries Cup](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/587444), Christmas Oguri can make the most of [Festive Miracle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572790) as all three of its effects (velocity, acceleration, recovery) are active. She can work in any style, but **Pace Chaser** should be the easiest to work with. Her best teams have double Front Runners or double [Dominator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922)debuffers once again to ensure victory.

Like in the Aries Cup, properly built Front Runners can still defeat Christmas Oguri Cap in the Cancer Cup. Among them, the strongest picks are [Summer Maruzensky](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/556411) and [Valentine Mihono Bourbon](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/580437). Make sure to train them with key skills such as early acceleration skills, good mid-race, and [Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060). You'll also want to run at least 2 of them together so they can pull ahead.

Most of the other styles aren't expected to be as strong, mostly due to the strength of Fronts and Christmas Oguri. Pace doesn't have good late acceleration options outside of [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894), and [Head On](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564783)[/Neck and Neck](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/594185) may be too random.

Late and End can continue using the combo of [Moving Past, and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534), [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), and **No Stopping Me**, although catching up to Christmas Oguri may prove to be difficult.

・**2 Fronts + 1 Any** - Front-leaning composition. 2 Fronts guarantee support for each other. The 3rd ace can be Christmas Oguri or another Front. Runaway can be used for rabbit/sacrificial strategies.

・**Christmas Oguri + 2 Debuffers** - Common strategy from previous Medium CMs. Reduces chances of getting blocked by Fronts. [Dominator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536922) is the debuff of choice.

・**3 Late/Ends** - A gambling-focused composition. Ends may be better due to their faster late-race speed.

As the late-race of CM15 starts on a corner, the usual legacy accelerations ([Angling and Scheming](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550060), [Let's Pump Some Iron](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543235), and [Moving Past, and Beyond](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572534)) are all highly recommended for their respective styles. [No Stopping Me!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536894) or even [Nimble Navigator](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536987) should also be considered by all styles where they can have a rival ahead of them.

The random skills ([Head-On](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/564783), [Slick Surge](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536973), [Take the Chance](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/591704)) are all usable if you have no other options, but they're lower priority picks since the options above are better in general. [Red Shift](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536883) is another low priority pick as it will technically work, but activates later than ideal.

Due to Christmas Oguri Cap's presence, **strong mid-race velocity** skills will be important to get into good positions. **Corner velocity skills** and [It's On!](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541561)/[Ramp Up](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/537007) are great picks for all styles. Strong rare skills include [Killer Tunes](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536928) (Front/Pace), [Fast & Furious](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536898) (Late), and [Daring Strike](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/577265) (End). [Uma Stan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536961) will also be key for Late/End to get ahead early on.

Late-race inherited skills like [Triumphant Pulse](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536882), [Shadow Break](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/545532), and [Behold Thine Emperor's Divine Might](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536889) are also nice to have for a last chance to catch up in the last spurt.

The necessity of getting recovery skills largely depends on your available support cards, Stamina, and Guts. As noted above, around 850 Stamina with 500 Guts should be enough without any recovery. However, you can **learn a rare recovery skill** to reduce the stamina needed or for extra safety against Debuffers.

[Oguri Cap (Ashen Miracle)](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572578) requires **3 recovery skills** to activate [Festive Miracle](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/572790). It's important to have **Triple 7s** since this will allow her unique to become a **strong acceleration skill**. She can also aim for around **600-650 Stamina** instead and focus more on **increasing Wit** to ensure her recoveries work. Unlike CM12, however, [U=ma2](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/543236) isn't as strong of a backup recovery, but can substitute any of the non-Triple 7s skills.

It is a very powerful card for the [Trackblazer Scenario](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/580723), however, its useful life is very short as it isn't used in future scenarios. Our deck recommendations will include this card due to its strength, but we **highly recommend borrowing it** rather than pulling for it if you're saving your Carats for the July banners.

If you're preparing ahead of the Cancer Cup, this card can be substituted with a **Wit Card**, a **Power Card**, or [Riko Kashimoto SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/561497). The Throne SSR primarily gives Wit and Speed, so decks using this may want to invest in more **Stamina sparks** to meet the requirement.

For Speed skills in general, [Yaeno Muteki SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/541221) is an excellent parent card, providing a lot of strong velocity skills for all styles. There's also [King Halo Power SSR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/550087), which can give [Homestretch Haste](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536977) and [Uma Stan](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536961). For an accessible option, [Agnes Tachyon Wit SR](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536680) is a great pick for both [Up-Tempo](https://game8.co/games/Umamusume-Pretty-Derby/archives/536970) and Medium velocity skills.

Try to aim for at least 600 of the stat you wish to pass down to get a chance for a 3-star spark. You can also replace the cards with lower rarity versions if you're only after the random skill hints.
