Personagens recomendados:
  - Oguri Cap (Starlight Beat)
      ・Easy to train due to aptitude in Mile and Medium. ・Has a strong last spurt acceleration skill by default (Triumphant Pulse). ・Becomes significantly stronger when inheriting other late-race acceleration skills like Red Shift/LP1211-M and Shooting for Victory! ・Best suited as a Pace Chaser.
  - Maruzensky (Formula R)
      ・Is the best parent Uma for passing down Red Shift/LP1211-M to Front Runner/Pace Chaser trainees. ・Noticeably easy career like Oguri Cap, proficient already in Medium and Mile. ・Must built as a strong Front Runner to be effective (skills that bring you quickly to the lead like Groundwork will help greatly).
  - Gold Ship (Red Strife)
      ・Oneo f the strongest End Closers, able to carry the race in case the Front Runner or Pace Chaser of the race falls back. ・Best built at Potential Lvl. 5 where the recovery skill Go-Home Specialist becomes available. ・Best to boost late-race acceleration further with Behold Thine Emperor's Divine Might and Let's Pump Some Iron! (Feel the Burn!).
  - Nice Nature (Poinsettia Ribbon)
      ・One the most efficient Debuffers for Medium. Has several debuffs as part of its Potential Level unlocks, notably Mystifying Murmur and All-Seeing Eyes. ・Does not require to have very high stats to compete. You only need to make sure she has high Wit to proc her skills (above 500 is recommended) and high enough Stamina to catch up with the pack in front of her as a Late Surger.

Acceleration
Unlike previous iterations of CM, few Inherited Acceleration Skills are viable on this Track. The Final Corner lies entirely outside of the late-race, rendering traditional Corner-based Acceleration such as Let's Pump Some Iron , Angling and Scheming , and Shooting for Victory ineffective.
Instead, Final Straight Acceleration Skills become viable. Two examples are Victoria por plancha ☆ and Give Mummy a Hug ♡ : Although these Skills are traditionally poor inherits due to their Hybrid nature, there are no better Accel Unique Inherits for this Track. As these Skills have front positional requirements, they can be difficult to properly utilize on Backliners.
A possible alternative for Backliners is Red Shift/LP1211-M . While this Skill activates when positioned 5th or better on the Final Corner and beyond, Backliners can delay the activation timing of Red Shift by moving up into 5th at or after the 4th Corner using Corner and Final Corner Velocity Skills.
Another possible consideration are non-Inherited Acceleration Skills. As the late-race starts immediately on a straight, Straightaway Spurt is a viable option for End Closers. Phase_random==2 Skills, such as Head-On for Pace Chasers and Ignited Spirit: PWR are mostly consistent due to the long time it takes to accelerate to Top Speed. As the only Uphill on the Track occurs during the Accel Zone, Highlander is a viable Accel Skill.
Often, Skills that activate later in the Accel Zone will contribute fully to Accelerating in Chukyo 1200m.
All that remains are Gambling Accels, such as Turbo Sprint , Rushing Gale , On Your Left! and Plan X . While On Your Left! is not as recommended due to positional requirements making it even more inconsistent, White versions of the other Skills can be taken: For example, Straightaway Acceleration is a semi-consistent Skill that can be taken to provide another win condition. It is generally not recommended to purchase the full Gold Skill as it is too SP-intensive.
Recommended Velocity Skills
Velocity Skills that trigger in the mid-race are desirable in any CM to help fight for positions, minimise losses, and widen leads. Some types of Skills are better than others this CM.
Any Corner Velocity Skills are highly desirable on this track. Skills such as Professor of Curvature , Style Corners , or Distance Corners will help secure positional requirements for the two key Acceleration Skills, Victoria por plancha ☆ and Give Mummy a Hug ♡. They can also potentially Speed Carryover , and will never lose effectiveness from activating in the Accel Zone.
Corner Velocity Skills
Skills that trigger on the Final Corner are especially effective as they will trigger after Position Keep, ensuring that the Skill is fully effective. These Skills are also more likely to Carryover. Some examples are Prepared to Pass , Outer Swell , or inherits such as Shadow Break , The Duty of Dignity Calls , and Nemesis . While Acceleration Uniques should be prioritized over Final Corner ones, they are good secondary options.
Straight Velocity Skills are far lower priority than Corner Skills. This is because Straight Skills are highly likely to trigger during either the early-race Acceleration or the late-race Accel Zone, rendering them ineffective.
Any other generic Target Speed increase remains effective.

Aquarius Cup
Aquarius Cup, or the 10th Champions Meeting (CM10), takes place on the Tokyo 1600m Dirt Track, which is Left-Handed, Firm, Sunny, and during the Winter.
CM10 Infographic
Acceleration
Aquarius Cup has very standard Acceleration conditions, with a late-race that starts off on the final Corner and transitions into a Straight in 33 meters. This allows us to use Acceleration Skills that proc on the final Corner or any Corner in the late-race, such as Angling and Scheming and Let's Pump Some Iron! .
CM10 Track
As the final Straight is only 33m into the Accel Zone, we can also use Acceleration Skills that proc on Straights. Similar to last CM, the most viable Frontline inherited Accels that activate on Straights are Victoria por plancha ☆ and Give Mummy a Hug ♡ . Unlike last CM, however, there are more options for Accel Skills: These two Skills are not as mandatory as they were in Capricorn Cup. Similarly, Straightaway Spurt is once again viable.
Similar to the last CM, Red Shift/LP1211-M is a Skill that can be considered on Backliners. Because this Skill procs when positioned 5th or better on the Final Corner and beyond, Backliners can delay the activation timing of Red Shift by moving up into 5th at or after the 4th Corner. However, as this can be inconsistent, Red Shift should only be considered as a secondary Accel.
WARNING
This strategy mainly works on Red Shift because of the Skill’s lenient Activation timing. Skills such as Shooting for Victory have very strict Activation Conditions, making it difficult for Umas to move into position to properly utilize the Acceleration.
The final Unique Accel Skill to consider is Shooting for Victory! . Although Shooting for Victory! activates too early when inherited, Taiki Shuttle’s Shooting for Victory! lasts 5 seconds on this track, and slightly overlaps with the Accel Zone.
Inherited Shooting for Victory versus Taiki’s innate Unique
All that remains are the Gambling Accels. All phase_random==2 Skills, such as Head-On , Ignited Spirit PWR , Forward, March! , and Updrafters can be considered for secondary Acceleration Skills. In addition, as the only Uphill of the Track is in the Accel Zone, Highlander is also a viable Gambling Accel. Late Surgers have access to Slick Surge and On Your Left! , which is not recommended due to strict position requirements making the Skill even more inconsistent.
Recommended Velocity Skills
The defining feature of the Tokyo 1600m Dirt Track is the long Straight immediately followed by the wide mid-race Corners. There are a few factors to consider with this Track’s layout.
The mid-race of the Track consists of a 383 meter Straight, then a 417 meter Corner. The Corner in the mid-race is the first Corner of the Track, which means that 200 meters out of the 383 meter Straight is a No-Zone. Combined with the fact that it is fundamentally more difficult to overtake on Corners due to Wide Swinging, most of the overtakes must happen in the early- and early mid-race.
TIP
Because Tokyo is the widest racecourse, it is especially difficult to overtake on the corners.
Due to this, Velocity Skills that proc in the early-race are valuable for fighting for position. Some examples are Leader's Pride , Productive Plan , and Uma Stan . In addition, combined with the long initial Straight, the Lane Movement Combo is very valuable for Front Runners.
WARNING
Note that Productive Plan is a phase_random Skill that cannot activate in the first 5 seconds. Due to this, it has around a 25% chance to not activate.
Skill Proc Data from Kachisim
Because of the difficulty in overtaking, defensive Skills to secure positioning are also valuable, especially in the final Corner. Examples are Skills that activate when an Uma is close behind or to the side, such as Playtime's Over! , Top Pick and Leader's Pride . Speed Eater is also a very strong skill that can stop any overtake attempt in its tracks.
Backliners often need to overtake on the Corners in order to secure positional requirements for Acceleration Skills after Position Keep ends. To achieve this successfully, stacking Corner Skills becomes necessary. Standard Skills such as Style or Distance Corners and Professor of Curvature are good, and Inherited Uniques such as Shadow Break , Nemesis , and The Duty of Dignity Calls serve as mid-race Skills in this Track. The Overtake Combo of Slipstream , Ramp Up , and Tail Held High can also be considered.
After accelerating to Top Speed in the late-race, the remainder of the Track is a long Straight. Due to this, final Straight/late-race Velocity Skills remain valuable for final overtakes and securing the lead. Skills that activate on a random Straight have a chance to proc in the early- and late-race, providing two helpful use cases.
As always, generic Velocity Skills remain effective.
Recommended Stats
Compared to several previous Tracks, Tokyo 1600m Dirt has a relatively shorter Downhill, with a long Uphill in the late-race. While the impact of the Uphill’s Target Speed penalty is minimal due to most of it occurring within the Accel Zone, the lowered Acceleration still makes Power a more favorable Stat over Wit.
Recommended Stats
The Stamina and Guts required to survive depends on the Style played. While Late Surgers can get away with Stamina/Guts as low as 500/500, Pace Chasers require slightly more Stamina at around 550. Front Runners also require around 550 Stamina and 500 Guts due to Spot Struggling.
It's very reasonable to hit the 601 threshold for an additional Speed Bonus.

A potential replacement for Riko are Power Cards that provide important Skills. Some examples are Smart Falcon for Groundwork , Top Pick , and Prudent Positioning , and Daitaku Helios for Speed Eater , Mile Corners ◎ , and Escape Artist . End Closers can also consider Admire Vega for Daring Strike and a plethora of End Closer Skills.

Pisces Cup
Pisces Cup, or the 11th Champions Meeting (CM11), takes place on the Hanshin 3200m Turf Track. The Conditions are as shown on the Champions Meeting page (Right, Heavy, Rain, Spring).
Also check out our Trackblazer Guide !
CM11 Pisces Cup Infographic
TIP
Despite being based on the Tenno Sho (Spring), Pisces Cup occurs on the Hanshin Racecourse due to the Kyoto Racecourse being under renovation in 2022, when this Champions Meeting occurred originally.
When making Practice Rooms for the Pisces Cup, make sure to make a Hanshin 3200m Room instead of selecting Tenno Sho (Spring)!
The proper conditions for a CM11 Practice Room
Acceleration
Similar to other Long Tracks, Hanshin 3200m starts its Accel Zone on a Straight. This allows us to use Acceleration Skills that proc on a late-race Straight such as Straightaway Spurt . However, as this is not the final Straight, Skills such as Victoria por plancha ☆ are not viable as Acceleration Skills.
CM11 Track
The first late-race Corner is 117 meters delayed, and renders all Corner-based Accel Skills such as Let's Pump Some Iron! and Angling and Scheming invalid. These Skills activate too late to have a meaningful impact.
The most universal Acceleration Skill is Nimble Navigator / No Stopping Me! . The Accel Zone begins before the final Corner, making it easier for these two Skills to activate. However, as No Stopping Me! is no longer universally provided in Trackblazer, acquiring these Skills may be difficult.
A new generic consideration for Acceleration in Trackblazer is Radiant Star . Radiant Star is a Scenario-exclusive Skill that provides Velocity, Acceleration, and Recovery for 1.2 Seconds. Because of this Skill’s inconsistent Activation Conditions and high SP Cost, relying on Radiant Star is not recommended.
Front Runners
The main Acceleration for Front Runners is inherited Victory Cheer! , Kitasan Black’s Unique. The Skill only provides .1 Accel when inherited: However, as Angling and Scheming is NOT viable on Hanshin 3200m, there are no better options.
INFO
Kitasan Black’s Unique, Victory Cheer! , will only provide Acceleration when positioned at the Backstretch late-race.
The Backstretch is defined as a Straight that is on the opposite side of the Racecourse as the final Straight. Simply put, Kitasan Black’s Unique will only provide Acceleration if the late-race begins on a Straight that is not the final Straight, a characteristic mainly found on longer Long Tracks.
The Arima Kinen, or Nakayama 2500m, has a late-race that begins on a Straight that is not the final Straight. In this situation, Kitasan Black’s Unique will provide Acceleration.
Pace Chasers
Pace Chasers, similar to Front Runners, rely on Victory Cheer! as their primary source of Acceleration. While Head-On remains a viable Acceleration Skill, it is less effective than it was in the previous two Champions Meetings.
WARNING
Because of the positional requirements for Victory Cheer! , it may be difficult for Pace Chasers to reliably activate this Skill. Due to the lack of a consistent Accel option, it is not recommended to play this Style in Pisces Cup without a proper plan.
Late Surgers
Outside of universal Acceleration Skills, the only option for Acceleration Late Surgers have is On Your Left! . Due to the longer Accel Zone in Long Tracks, it is even more unlikely that On Your Left! will be effective and is not recommended.
End Closers
Because the late-race begins on a Straight, End Closers have access to a consistent Acceleration Skill in Straightaway Spurt .
Velocity Skills
Velocity Skills that activate on Straights have additional value for several reasons. First of all, because the late-race begins on a Straight, most random or mid-race Straight Skills have a chance to Carryover. However, unlike Skills such as Speed Star or The Duty of Dignity Calls that can semi-consistently Carryover when the Track allows for it, this is an uncommon occurrence and should not be relied on.
In addition, as overtaking on a Straight is easier than overtaking around Corners, Straight Skills can be used to gain position in the mid-race. Unlike many previous Champions Meetings, there are no position-based Accels that will punish pushing up too far in the mid-race. However, as there are four separate Straights throughout the Track, random Straight Skills cannot be relied on to consistently activate in the mid-race.
Because the duration of Skills scale with the length of the Track, Skills with longer duration have priority over shorter Skills. In Pisces Cup, Skills will have their duration multiplied by 3.2x. This means that Skills that have a base duration of 3 seconds will have a 9.6 second duration, while a Skill with 1.8 second base duration will have a 5.76 second duration.
In practice, when deciding between an upgrade to Long Straightaways ◎ versus purchasing Ramp Up , the upgrade can be higher value due to the 3.84 second duration difference outvaluing the 0.05 Velocity difference.
The Straightaway Skill has much more value over Ramp Up in the Long Race compared to the Mile Race.
Recommended Stats
Recommended Stats for CM11
Compared to previous Champions Meetings, the Stamina requirements for Pisces Cup is exceptionally high. On top of the 3200 meters, HP drain is increased through the Heavy Ground condition and there is only one small Downhill before the late-race to alleviate further HP consumption.
INFO
The game calculates the amount of HP needed to complete the Race when entering the late-race to determine whether the Uma can fully Spurt. While there is a Downhill in the late-race, it is not included in this calculation: Because of this, while it is unlikely that an Uma will fully die, they will often fail to fully Spurt.
Generically speaking, 1100 Stamina along with a Gold and White Recovery Skill will be sufficient for this Track. However, based on the Style, more or less Stamina can be considered: For example, Front Runners and Pace Chasers require more Stamina due to Spot Struggling and poor Stamina to HP conversion, respectively. On the other hand, Late Surgers and End Closers require less Stamina due to a better conversion.
Power is very important in CM11 due to the limited availability of Acceleration Skills and the -50 reduction from the Heavy Ground Condition, while the 1000+ meters of Downhill and the increased number of Skills acquired in Trackblazer make Wit equally important.
Due to the high Stamina requirements and the long Homestretch in Hanshin 3200m, Guts is also an important Stat. While it shouldn’t be prioritized, having at least 600 will alleviate HP consumption in the late-race and provide a small edge while Dueling in the long final Straight.
Recommended Decks
Refer to our Trackblazer Guide for general Deck Advice!
The recommended Deck for Pisces Cup is the standard 2 SPD 2 WIT 2 Flex Deck. Using Kitasan Black as one of the Speed Cards is recommended for Professor of Curvature , which can potentially activate twice on this Track.
Due to the Stamina requirements, using at least one, up to two, Stamina Cards is recommended. Super Creek is the go-to Stamina Card for both Stats and Swinging Maestro . Manhattan Cafe is the best SR Stamina Card, providing both 10 Race Bonus and hints for valuable Skills such as Wet Conditions ○ , Stamina Eater , and Long Straightaways ○ .
Alternatively, Cards such as Narita Brian can be considered for Beeline Burst , a Gold Velocity Skill which can potentially activate twice on this Track. MLB SSR Riko Kashimoto can also be considered over a second Stamina Card. If running several Stamina Sparks, a Power Card can also be used over a second Stamina Card.
A second Stamina Card can be replaced with a Card for its Skill, such as Admire Vega for Daring Strike on End Closers. Keep in mind that this will make it more difficult to reach Stamina requirements, and should be done with prudence.

As Corner Acceleration Skills are not viable in CM11, Umas with Straight Acceleration Skills are valuable. Uma with Late-Race Velocity Uniques
Because of Hanshin 3200m’s long final Corner and Straight, Umas with strong late-race Velocity Uniques can close large gaps.
Uma with Mid-Race Velocity Uniques
Due to the scarcity of Acceleration Skills and the position-agnostic nature of existing ones, Umas that can gain a positional advantage in the mid-race will have an easier time securing the win. Fighting from a forward position is easier than fighting from behind.
Other Notable Uma
Umas with Accel or Hybrid Uniques that are not Track-dependent are valuable due to potential Acceleration where alternatives are scarce. Even if Hybrid Uniques do not overlap with the Accel Zone, they provide useful mid-race Velocity.

Aries Cup
Aries Cup, or the 12th Champions Meeting (CM12), takes place on the Nakayama 2000m Track (Right, Firm, Sunny, Spring).
CM12 Aries Cup Infographic
INFO
For players attempting to complete the Classic Triple Crown, this will be the last opportunity to run the Satsuki Sho until CM23, which is the first CM of Grandmasters.
Acceleration
After three back-to-back CMs where the best inherited Acceleration Skills were 0.1, CM12 offers a change of pace by starting the late-race on Corner 3. This means that all Skills that activate on late-race Corners, such as Angling and Scheming , Moving Past, and Beyond , and Let's Pump Some Iron! are viable.
WARNING
Depending on lobby composition, Moving Past, and Beyond and Let's Pump Some Iron! may be difficult to activate. These Skills have a positional requirement for 5-6th and 6th, respectively: If there are many Debuffers or Backliners, Umas can get easily shoved out of position for these Uniques.
CM12 Track
As both the final Corner and Straight are heavily delayed, Skills that activate on these Track Features such as Red Shift/LP1211-M and Straightaway Spurt are not recommended.
Outside of inherited Accels, the most universal Acceleration Skill available is Nimble Navigator / No Stopping Me! . Similar to last CM, the Accel Zone begins before the final Corner, making it easier for these two Skills to activate.
WARNING
Outside of Umas such as Mayano Top Gun who have the Skill through Potentials, No Stopping Me! can only be acquired through the use of Yukino Bijin , a weak Wit SSR. If the rest of the Deck is not strong enough to make up for using this Card, borrowing a stronger Card and acquiring Nimble Navigator through other means is recommended.
Christmas Oguri
An Uma that will be extremely prevalent during CM12 is Oguri Cap . Christmas Oguri’s Unique, Festive Miracle , provides .25 Velocity, .3 Acceleration, and 3.5% Recovery when three Recovery Skills are used. In Tracks ranging from 1800m to 2200m, Christmas Oguri can both get a guaranteed Speed Carryover and Acceleration from her Unique in the late-race through the use of Triple 7s .
Festive Miracle with Triple 7s
Another Skill that can be used to activate Christmas Oguri’s Unique in CM12 is U=ma2 , inherited from Agnes Tachyon. U=ma2 activates on the first Corner past the halfway point if the Uma is either 3rd or 4th: In CM12, this Skill will activate at the 1200m mark, only 23 meters before Triple 7s .
Festive Miracle with U=ma2
Utilizing both of these Uniques in a 4 Recovery Skill build will significantly decrease the chances of failing to activate Christmas Oguri’s Unique due to wit checks.
WARNING
While it should not be taken regardless, Radiant Star should especially not be taken on Christmas Oguri. Not only is it a bad Skill for the SP Cost, it can also cause Festive Miracle to activate early and miss the Accel Zone.
DANGER
Festive Miracle should NOT be inherited.
Recommended Stats
Recommended Stats for CM12
Compared to the previous CM, the Stamina requirement is very low, only requiring around 700 Stamina and 500 Guts. With more Guts, the Stamina requirement can also be slightly lowered. In addition, due to the lower Stamina requirement, acquiring additional Power and Wit is much easier. Many players will be able to cap these Stats, but some Stats can alternatively be forgone for more Races.
While Guts is not absolutely critical, having higher Guts is recommended due to the ease of hitting the cap on other Stats. As many people will be running triple-capped Umas, having higher Guts can help decide Races through Dueling.

Taurus Cup
Taurus Cup, or the 13th Champions Meeting (CM13), brings us back to the first CM track, Tokyo 2400m (Left, Firm, Sunny, Spring).
This CM is based off of the Japanese Derby: For players attempting to complete the Classic Triple Crown, this will be the last opportunity to run the Japanese Derby until CM31, the last CM in the U.A.F., Ready, GO! Scenario.
CM13 Taurus Cup Infographic
Acceleration
Similar to the previous CM, the late-race begins on Corner 3. This means that every corner-based Acceleration Skill, such as Angling and Scheming , Let's Pump Some Iron! , and Moving Past, and Beyond are viable.
One key difference in this CM is that the final Corner is only 25 meters delayed. Therefore, Maruzensky ’s Unique Skill, Red Shift/LP1211-M , can also be taken. Due to its lenient Positional Requirement of 1-5, it should be taken regardless of Style.
WARNING
Depending on lobby composition, Moving Past, and Beyond and Let's Pump Some Iron! may be difficult to activate. These Skills have a positional requirement for 5-6th and 6th, respectively: If there are many Debuffers or Backliners, Umas can get easily shoved out of position for these Uniques.
In addition, due to the Corner 3 only being 25 meters into the late-race, Umas must be fifth or sixth as they enter late-race in order to activate Moving Past, and Beyond , making the Activation Condition more strict.
CM13 Track
As the Straight is heavily delayed, Skills that activate on these Track Features such as Straightaway Spurt are not recommended.
Outside of inherited Accels, the most universal Acceleration Skill available is Nimble Navigator / No Stopping Me! . While this Skill is still somewhat consistent, it is less so than the previous CM due to the proximity of the final Corner to the start of late-race.
WARNING
Outside of Umas such as Mayano Top Gun who have the Skill through Potentials, No Stopping Me! can only be acquired through the use of Yukino Bijin , a weak Wit SSR. If the rest of the Deck is not strong enough to make up for using this Card, borrowing a stronger Card and acquiring Nimble Navigator through other means is recommended.
Velocity Skills
There are two Velocity Skills that are important to consider for CM13: Uma Stan and 1,500,000 CC .
Similar to many previous CMs, CM13 is on a Track that is longer than 2200m. This means that Section 1 takes longer than 5 seconds to exit, and Uma Stan will cancel PDM when activated immediately. Although the effect of this PDM cancel is much lower than the previous Long CMs, the early No-Zone makes any and all leads gained early important.
A more interesting Skill is 1,500,000 CC , a Late Surger Skill that can activate at any Uphill. Because Tokyo 2400m has three Uphills, one of which occurs immediately as the Race starts, there is a one in three chance that 1,500,000 CC activates at the start of the Race, instantly breaking Gate PDM and providing a large lead over everyone else. Strong Front Runners or Runaways can be used to then preserve this lead.
Even if the Skill doesn’t activate on the first Uphill, the second Uphill occurs immediately after the end of Position Keep, ensuring that the Velocity Skill is fully utilized. The final Uphill occurs in the late-race, supplementing much-needed late-race Velocity.
WARNING
Velocity Skills that activate on the final Corner, such as Speed Star or The Duty of Dignity Calls , will likely trigger while the Uma is still accelerating. Since she will still be accelerating, the increased Target Speed will mostly go to waste.
Recommended Stats
CM13 Recommended Stats
Compared to the previous CM, the Stamina requirement is very high, necessitating around 800 Stamina and a Gold Recovery Skill. Front Runners should have slightly more Stamina, while Late Surgers and End Closers can have slightly less.
Stamina is especially important this CM, as the long final Straight will mean that Umas will be dueling for a long duration. Dueling can only be entered while having more than 15% HP, and will be exited when dropping below 5%. For the same reason, higher Guts is also recommended.
In lieu of a proper Gold Recovery, Radiant Star is acceptable if you are slightly above the recommended Stamina. There is a slight risk it may proc too late to be useful, but these odds are low enough that you may benefit more from bringing a stronger Deck.

Gemini Cup
Gemini Cup, or the 14th Champions Meeting (CM14), takes place in the same Track as the original Cancer Cup, or CM3. This CM is based off of the Yasuda Kinen.
Acceleration
Unlike the previous CMs, the late-race begins on the final Corner, with the final Straight following 33 meters after. Uniques that activate on any late-race Corner, such as Angling and Scheming and Let's Pump Some Iron! , will still function. However, Moving Past, and Beyond and Red Shift/LP1211-M activate at the start of Corner 4, and activate too early to overlap with the late-race.
Similar to CM10 , Taiki Shuttle ’s Unique, Shooting for Victory! , works on Taiki Shuttle herself. However, the Skill does not overlap with the late-race when inherited due to the shortened duration.
Two Uniques that are newly viable to this Track are Nishino Flower ’s Unique, Budding Blossom , and King Halo ’s Unique, Louder! Tracen Cheer! . Budding Blossom has similar Activation Conditions to Shooting for Victory! , but with the caveat that it can only activate in the late-race at any point past the midpoint of Corner 4. This means that, unlike Shooting for Victory! , Budding Blossom will activate perfectly.
Like Shooting for Victory! , Budding Blossom also has a Positional Requirement of 3rd-4th. This Skill should be mainly taken on Pace Chasers, unless specific gameplans are made around bringing Late Surgers and End Closers into 4th in the mid-race.
WARNING
While Budding Blossom has looser Activation Conditions than Shooting for Victory! , the Skill also has a Precondition of being blocked on the side by 2 or more seconds in a mid-race Corner. This can make the Skill inconsistent depending on lobby composition.
Louder! Tracen Cheer! is a Unique that activates on the final Straight. While the final Straight is delayed by 33 meters, the distance is short enough to consider this Skill. Although Louder! Tracen Cheer! has no Positional Requirements, it also has very strict Preconditions of being unable to push past 6th in the first half of the Race. Due to this, it should mainly be taken on End Closers.
Outside of inherited Accels, the main consistent Acceleration Skill is Straightaway Spurt for End Closers. Due to the Accel Zone starting after the final Corner, Nimble Navigator / No Stopping Me! is inconsistent and only Nimble Navigator should be considered, at most.
Similar to previous Mile CMs, gambling Accels can be considered for secondary Accel Skills. Two of the most effective gambling Accels are Corner Acceleration ○ for Backliners and Final Push for Front Runners. As there are only two Corners on this Track, Corner Acceleration ○ has a high chance to activate in or near the Accel Zone, with around 20 to 22% effectiveness. Final Push only activates on the final Corner, which means that it has approximately double the effectiveness for around 40 to 44%.
INFO
Effectiveness was calculated as the chance to gain at least one second of Acceleration from the Skill in the late-race. The values will slightly increase based on the number of Velocity Skills activated within the Activation zone.
WARNING
While Corner Acceleration ○ / Corner Connoisseur can be used from any Position and therefore is effective for overtaking, Final Push can only be used from 1st and should mainly be taken as a hedge against Backliners.
In addition, it is often better to forego gambling Accels for consistent Velocity Skills, which can more consistently secure existing win conditions. Take these Skills with prudence.
Velocity Skills
Like CM10, CM14 is a very hostile environment for overtaking. The mid-race begins on a 283 meter Straight, into 517 meter Corners. This means that 200 meters out of the 283 meters Straight takes place in the No-Zone, and the remainder of the mid-race takes place around Corners.
TIP
Because Tokyo has the widest lanes, it is difficult to overtake around Corners.
Due to this, the early-race is especially important for securing position. Velocity Skills such as Unyielding Spirit , Leader's Pride , Productive Plan , and generic Straight Skills will help secure the lead early in addition to the Lane Movement Combo for Front Runners. Skills that defend position around Corners, such as Playtime's Over! , Speed Eater , and generic Corner Skills are also important.
Backliners will often need to aim to overtake around the Corners after the end of Position Keep to secure position for their Accels. For End Closers, Skills that activate in the final Corner such as Nemesis , Shadow Break , and Full of Vigor are exceptionally important as they will not overshoot Louder! Tracen Cheer! preconditions. These Skills also have a chance of providing Speed Carryover, which is especially effective in shorter Tracks.
As always, generic Velocity Skills remain effective.
Recommended Stats
As CM14 takes place in a short Mile Track, the Stamina requirements are on the lower side. However, as the Track Stat threshold is Stamina and Guts, at least 601 Stamina should be aimed for. If accounting for Bad Mood, aim for 613 Stamina instead.
Similar to the previous CM, Guts is especially effective on Backliners due to dueling on the long Final Straight. As Front Runners can’t consistently duel, Guts is slightly less effective: However, the additional lengths gained early on from Guts-boosted Spot Struggles can often be the difference between losing and securing 1st.
Recommended Decks and Support Cards
Refer to our Trackblazer Guide for general Deck Advice!
The recommended Deck for CM14 is the standard 1 SPD 3 GUT 2 WIT Deck. This base composition can be changed flexibly depending on the Growths of the Uma: For example, Umas with Guts growths can opt to drop a Guts Card in exchange for another Wit or Speed Card.
The three standard Guts Cards for budget Players is Haru Urara , Yukino Bijin , and Admire Vega . Alternatives include Sirius Symboli , Air Groove , and Seeking the Pearl .
INFO
At this point, Players will have three SR Uncap Crystals if none were missed or used. Due to the importance of Admire Vega Guts, using all 3 on it is recommended if it is at least 1LB.
Alternate Guts Cards to consider are Ines Fujin for her high Specialty Priority and Hints on valuable Skills, Bamboo Memory for Full of Vigor , and Mejiro Palmer for the Power Bonus.
WARNING
Ines Fujin and Mejiro Palmer , while powerful Cards, only have 5 Race Bonus.
As Guts Decks are fundamentally more volatile, Players can also opt to run SPD PWR WIT Decks like previous CMs.

Cancer Cup
Cancer Cup, or the 15th Champions Meeting (CM15), takes place on the Hanshin 2200m Track. This CM is based on the Takarazuka Kinen , which was previously run in the first Leo Cup (CM4).
Acceleration
Similar to most Medium CMs, CM15 is a Corner 3 Acceleration Track. This means that all generic Corner Acceleration Skills, such as Angling and Scheming and Let's Pump Some Iron! , are viable. Corner 3 Acceleration Skills, such as Moving Past, and Beyond , are viable as well.
WARNING
Depending on lobby composition, Moving Past, and Beyond and Let's Pump Some Iron! may be difficult to activate. These Skills have a positional requirement for 5-6th and 6th, respectively: If there are many Debuffers or Backliners, Umas can often find themselves out of position for these Uniques.
As both the final Corner and Straight are heavily delayed, Skills that activate on these Track Features such as Red Shift/LP1211-M and Straightaway Spurt are not recommended.
Outside of inherited Acceleration Uniques, the most universal Acceleration Skill available is Nimble Navigator / No Stopping Me! . Similar to last CM, the Accel Zone begins before the final Corner, making it easier for these two Skills to activate.
WARNING

Christmas Oguri
An Uma that will be seen often during CM15 is Oguri Cap . Christmas Oguri’s Unique, Festive Miracle , provides .25 Target Speed, .3 Acceleration, and 3.5% Recovery when three Recovery Skills are used. In Tracks ranging from 1800m to 2200m, Christmas Oguri can both get a guaranteed Speed Carryover and Acceleration from her Unique in the late-race through the use of Triple 7s .
WARNING
Unlike CM12, Corner 3 is too early for U=ma2 to cause Festive Miracles to carry over making it an invalid backup recovery. Due to this, Christmas Oguri only has a 79% chance to activate her Unique at 1200 Wit.
WARNING

INFO

── UMA.GUIDE (guia completo) ──
Leo Cup
LeoCup, or the 16th Champions Meeting (CM16), takes place on the Nakayama 1200m Track. This CM is based on the Sprinters Stakes .
Acceleration
This Sprint CM is very different from the previous Sprint CM: The Late-Race Accel Zone starts halfway through the Final Corner making previously effective Straight Acceleration Skills , such as Straightaway Acceleration / Rushing Gale! , Victoria por plancha ☆ , and Give Mummy a Hug ♡ , now ineffective Skills.
Instead, Budding Blossom and Shooting for Victory! are the most desirable Acceleration Skills for this track. These Skills will provide guaranteed value for all Uma in 3rd and 4th - they are so reliable, it would not be unusual for players to strategise entirely around fighting for these positions. This is best done with Pace Chasers equipped with a wide arsenal of Mid-Race Velocity Skills .
Other guaranteed positional Accels are Angling and Scheming for Front Runners or Pace Chasers, and Let's Pump Some Iron! for Late Surgers and End Closers. While it is not improbable for a Pace Chaser to activate Let's Pump Some Iron! due to the tense competition for 3rd and 4th, it is not recommended to have this Skill on Pace Chasers because a Pace Chaser in 6th has already lost the Race.
Securing the positional Accels will be the primary objective for all Uma, but there are a lot of secondary choices for Acceleration which can either make up for missing the positionals, or complement them well. For Pace Chasers competing for 3rd and 4th, these secondary Accels will be necessary to tie break against the other Uma.
Front Runners have access to Final Push / Unrestrained . Although this skill is usually unreliable on other tracks, it is very likely to provide value on Nakayama 1200.
INFO
The July 1st patch has also made Second Wind a viable Skill as complementary Accel for Front RUnners. However this should only be considered after other options are exhausted, since the probability of Second Wind activating outside the Accel Zone is fairly high.
TIP
Follow this link to read more about the Skill Adjustments made on the July 1st patch
Pace Chasers have access to Head-On / Neck and Neck , which now reliably provides value with the July 1st update.
Late Surgers have many options. The most accessible is Slick Surge / On Your Left! which was made reliable after the July 1st update. Preferably, Late Surgers should also have Leap Forward , a new Skill which provides Acceleration if an Uma passes another from the back half of the pack. This Skill is immensely valuable and will consistently put Late Surgeres in a winning position.
WARNING
As a recently introduced Skill with no respective Support Card, there is currently no way to obtain Leap Forward outside of lucky random Race hints in Trackblazer, or Parents that got it from lucky random Race hints in Trackblazer. Although Leap Forward is not strictly necessary, players who put in this effort will be greatly rewarded.
INFO
Red Shift/LP1211-M on its own has little to no value as an Accel. However, players can consider buying it as an extension to Let's Pump Some Iron! . This can be considerably expensive, especially since Maruzensky infamously has weak affinity with many Uma; This route is generally not recommended, but nonetheless it is a valid build if players can train their Late Surger with enough Mid-Race Skills to overcome the abundant Pace Chasers before Late-Race.
End Closers have the same tools as Late Surgers, minus Slick Surge / On Your Left! .

Corner Acceleration ○ / Corner Connoisseur ,
Nimble Navigator / No Stopping Me! ,

and Countermeasure .
Although universal, these Skills are less reliable due to their increased chances of activating outside of the Accel Zone. Nonetheless, they are valid secondary options to complement existing Accel. Meticulous Measures and Tactical Tweak are last resort, ill-advised Skills a player could purchase with Spare SP.
WARNING
Because Nimble Navigator and No Stopping Me! are given for free in the Unity Cup Scenario, players may be tempted into purchasing them first. It must be noted that these Skills are weak and not often effective on tracks that start Late-Race on or after the Final Corner. Nakayama 1200 is one of these tracks. Nimble Navigator could still be considered with Spare SP, but No Stopping Me! is often too expensive an opportunity cost for other Velocity Skills or Accels.
Velocity Skills
Nakayama 1200 has specific features which lay out the track in an interesting manner. The first 300 metres is neither a Straight nor a Corner, and the only Straight on the track is the Final Straight well into the Late-Race.
This allows us to split Velocity skills cleanly into two categories:
Corner Skills/Mid-Race
Corner Skills are almost always Mid-Race Skills. Generally speaking, Mid-Race Skills are of higher priority than Late-Race skills. Skills such as Professor of Curvature , Sprint Corners , and Style Corners will be necessary for Uma to fight for crucial Acceleration positions leading up to the Late-Race. Generically useful Mid-Race Skills such as In High Spirits , Burning Spirit SPD , It's On! , etc. also serve this purpose.
INFO
With how tight the pack in this CM is likely to be, Skills that activate on Overtake , in Overtake Mode when Challenged, or when being Overtaken are very effective in gaining or maintaining leads. These Skills include Leader's Pride / Top Runner , Ramp Up / It's On! , and Shadow Break .
Straight Skills/Late-Race Skills
Straight Skills are always Late-Race Skills. While they technically always provide Lengths and are very much key Skills for closing out the Race, they are only effective if the Uma has enough Acceleration to compete. These Skills should be considered only after purchasing enough Acceleration and Mid-Race Skills.
A unique exception to the above lies in Smart Falcon's SPARKLY☆STARDOM . Despite the Skill description saying it activates on a Straight, the actual in-game logic checks if Smart Falcon is “not-on-a-Corner”. This nuance allows SPARKLY☆STARDOM to be one of the only “Straight” Skills in the game to activate in the Mid-Race.

Análise da meta:
  As another 2000m Medium race, we're expecting Christmas Oguri Cap to be the dominant ace again, much like the Aries Cup (CM12). Just like that race, she has 2 good activators for Festive Miracle in Triple 7s and U=ma2, making it unlikely for her to fail to use her powerful unique. In addition, her unique also being a recovery makes her not have to worry about stamina debuffers, making her the most effective runner by far.
  Copano Rickey is very much hyped as a meta Dirt ace, and she can certainly prove her worth here. Although Christmas Oguri Cap is more consistent, Copano Rickey should be able to score some wins against her, provided her unique skill triggers as close to the start of the late-race as possible. You can even run both of them together to solidify your chances of winning, as they're both very strong contenders. Just make sure to give her 6 green skills for the race to maximize her unique's effect.
  Outside of these top two, Front Runners should be able to compete thanks to being able to use Angling and Scheming. Both versions of Smart Falcon are great picks as they're specifically built for Dirt tracks.

  As a Medium race with Christmas Oguri Cap, double Dominators are expected yet again. This time, they can also run 564 Escapades to also carry a second rare debuff like Keen Eye.

── UMA.GUIDE (guia completo) ──
Virgo Cup
Virgo Cup, or the 17th Champions Meeting (CM17), takes place on the Oi 2000m Track. This CM is based on the JBC Classic .
Acceleration
The Oi 2000m Track has similar Acceleration conditions to most other Medium CMs: late-race starts on the 3rd Corner. Acceleration inherits such as Angling and Scheming , Let's Pump Some Iron! , and Moving Past, and Beyond are effective. These will be the primary Acceleration Skills Front Runners, Late Surgers, and End Closers will fight for. The Final Corner is much further out, so Red Shift/LP1211-M , Shooting for Victory! , and Budding Blossom should not be taken.
Nimble Navigator is a complementary Accel Skill useful to all Uma besides Front Runners. However, unless you are training in Unity Cup, it is recommended to acquire this Skill via Inheritance because Deck space is expensive in Grand Concert. This also applies to the Gold version, No Stopping Me! .
Dirt CMs also have a few Accels players normally won’t see on Turf. Got the Spirit! / Be the Center! , Forward, March! / Lead the Charge! , and Rapid Gain can be considered if a player chances upon them via random Race Hints or Character Events. These Skills aren’t high priority purchases especially because they have fully random activations in the late-race - nonetheless, they could help round out an Uma’s build if they otherwise lack Accel.
TIP
The Final Corner of this track lies beyond the Acceleration Zone, so any Accel Skills activating in this section is typically wasted. However, the Skill Forward, March! has a unique secondary effect that makes it a more appealing option for reasons besides Acceleration: The Skill contains a Lane Movement bonus. The Final Corner is where Uma attempt to change lanes, meaning Lane Movement bonus will provide the Uma significant forward Velocity in this section of the Track. This makes Forward, March! effective in two ways: if it procs early it is an effective Acceleration Skill, if it procs late it is a solid Velocity Skill.
Other style-specific complementary Accel Skills include Head-On / Neck and Neck for Pace Chasers, Slick Surge / On Your Left! for Late Surgers, and Take the Chance / From the Brink for Late Surgers and End Closers. Second Wind is technically a viable complementary Accel for Front Runners, however the 2% HP Cost is often too steep a price for this CM when Stamina requirements are so strict.
Christmas Oguri Cap
Christmas Oguri Cap will appear frequently in this CM. Just like other tracks ranging from 1800m to 2200m, Christmas Oguri can get both a guaranteed Speed Carryover and Acceleration from Festive Miracle in the late-race through the use of Triple 7s .
TIP
Although occurring slightly later, U=ma2 is a reliable back-up proc for Christmas Oguri. It is recommended to take a total of four recoveries, two of them being Triple 7s and U=ma2 , when training Christmas Oguri.
WARNING
Festive Miracle should not be Inherited, as the Inherited version will not Carryover into the late-race.
Velocity Skills
This CM doesn’t have any standout features which make particular Skills excel more than others. Any Velocity Skill, as long as it activates, will be generically useful.
WARNING
There are no Downhill or Uphill features on the Oi Racecourse. Skills with these conditions such as 1,500,000 CC and Wherever This Wonder Leads will not activate

Virgo Cup will be particularly harsh when it comes to Stamina. Since there are no Downhills on the Oi Racecourse, the Stamina normally saved from Downhill Mode does not happen. All these factors drive the Stamina goal up to 800 Stamina and at least 1 Gold Recovery Skill, or over 1000 raw Stamina.
TIP
800 Stamina + 1 Gold Recovery Skill, or 1000 raw Stamina, is enough Stamina for Uma to survive two direct hits of Mystifying Murmur . Trainers may adjust their Stamina up or down accordingly if they believe they may encounter more or less Stamina Debuffers.
WARNING
While tempting to reach for the new Speed cap of 1600 Speed, players should always ensure they have enough Stamina to finish the race. If it means you can reach your Stam goals, having 100-200 less points of Speed may be a necessary trade.

Recommended Uma
The dominating Uma for this CM is expected to be Christmas Oguri Cap due to the reliability of Festive Miracle .
It will be tough to compete with other Uma as many of Christmas Oguri’s direct competitors require significantly more effort to train to an effective level.
One such Uma is Copano Rickey . Her Unique Luck Runs My Way is a hybrid Velocity and Acceleration Skill if Copano Rickey possesses 6 Green Passive Skills. True to its name, Luck Runs My Way could randomly activate in such a way that it Carries Over its hybrid effect into the late-race, allowing her to compete with Christmas Oguri Cap. Unfortunately, the effort required to get 6 Green Passive Skills is quite steep, and ultimately Luck Runs My Way is a tough gamble that doesn’t guarantee victory. Copano Rickey can be trained, in order of difficulty and performance, as a Late Surger, Front Runner, and Pace Chaser.
Otherwise, any other Dirt-capable Uma will fare decently in this CM. Agnes Digital and Inari One are both easily trainable Uma, each possessing useful innate Gold and White Skills so players can focus their efforts on training Stats.
WARNING
Besides Christmas Oguri Cap, Pace Chasers are the only Style that do not have an accessible Acceleration Skill, thus will be the weakest Style this CM. To have reliable Accel, Pace Chasers may need to be trained with Angling and Scheming to compete for Pole Position, but this is fairly difficult against Copano Rickey and Smart Falcon .
Debuffers
With the introduction of 564 Escapades , Debuffers have become an effective means to increase the performance floor of your Uma.
Uma with Mystifying Murmur can completely deplete opponent HP which would remove them from the race. Normally, Mystifying Murmur has a low chance to activate due to the difficult Block requirement - but 564 Escapades can guarantee its activation.
Since 564 Escapades can ignore Skill activation conditions on the target Skill, Uma with Dominator can be equipped with Keen Eye for an additional late-race Velocity Debuff.

  As a Mile race, acceleration skills will prove to be important in securing wins. All styles have good options, but keep in mind to avoid overspending skill points. Generally, you'll want to limit to 1 legacy/inherited acceleration and 1 rare skill, but the normal skill equivalent should be usable.

── UMA.GUIDE (guia completo) ──
Libra Cup
Libra Cup, or the 18th Champions Meeting (CM18), takes place on the Hanshin 1600m Track. This CM is based on the Mile Championship, which took place in Hanshin instead of Kyoto due to ongoing redevelopment during 2022.
Acceleration
This CM, the Late-race begins three-quarters of the way into the Final Corner, 60m before the Final Straight. As such, primary Acceleration Skills will be those which can activate on any part of the Corner - these are Angling and Scheming , Budding Blossom , and Let's Pump Some Iron! . The 60m delay relegates Straight Acceleration Skills like Straightaway Spurt and Victoria por plancha ☆ to weaker, complementary Skills and should only be taken if lacking Accel otherwise.
Red Shift/LP1211-M , Shooting for Victory! , and Moving Past, and Beyond activate too early in the Mid-race and are not recommended.
WARNING
Nimble Navigator / No Stopping Me! is typically not effective on tracks where the Late-race starts on or after the Final Corner. For CM18, this Skill should only be considered as a desperate last resort.
TIP
Acceleration is a Skill worth considering on Pace Chasers, Late Surgers, and End Closers. Although it should not be specifically targeted, this Skill can appear as a Lv5-Hint from Taiki Shuttle , or as a random Hint from Techniques in Grand Concert. Since the Skill has similar conditions to other vital Skills such as Shadow Break , Acceleration can serve as a cheap complement to if Players have spare SP.
Front Runners
As usual, Angling and Scheming is the primary Acceleration Skill for Front Runners. Usually Angling and Scheming is their only Accel, but the track features for this CM allow Final Push / Unrestrained to be an effective albeit somewhat random complementary Acceleration Skill. Meanwhile, Victoria por plancha ☆ is a more stable, but less effective, complementary option.
Second Wind is not recommended.
Pace Chasers
Pace Chasers have two viable pathways which can be fought for individually, or attempted simultaneously.
The first, easier path will be to inherit Budding Blossom onto the Pace Chaser. Budding Blossom is a fully effective instant Acceleration Skill on Hanshin 1600 provided the pre-condition is met.
WARNING
Unfortunately, Budding Blossom ’s pre-condition can fail because it is not guaranteed Pace Chasers will have another Uma by their side Mid-race. Even if the player enters the CM with two Pace Chasers, this won’t guarantee Budding Blossom 's activation because the track’s wide Corner typically results in Pace Chasers running in a line rather than side by side. Pace Chasers should have alternative Acceleration Skills to account for Budding Blossom failing.
The alternative, more difficult build involves equipping Pace Chasers with Angling and Scheming to fight for First against the Front Runners. This is enabled by stacking Final Corner Skills which include Speed Star , Shadow Break , The Duty of Dignity Calls , alongside Skills such as Mile Corners , Pace Chaser Corners , Professor of Curvature , Shifting Gears / Changing Gears , to attack the Front Runners before the Late-race. Angling and Scheming will also prove to be a very effective Skill in matches without a Front Runner present, since being promoted to Pacemaker will lock the Uma out of Budding Blossom anyway.
Both the above Acceleration Skills can be taken simultaneously, broadening the scope of the Pace Chaser’s win conditions at the cost of a tougher Lineage.
No matter the primary Accel, Pace Chasers will need one or two more secondary Accels to escape the back-liners behind them. Head-On / Neck and Neck , and Straight Descent / Determined Descent are each random Accels that may be effective this CM.
TIP
Although Shooting for Victory! is ineffective as an Inherited Skill, Shooting for Victory! is still effective as a Unique Skill on Taiki Shuttle herself. This makes Nishino Flower and Taiki Shuttle the only two Pace Chasers with effective +0.4 Acceleration this CM.
Late Surgers
Late Surgers are looking to have Let's Pump Some Iron! and Budding Blossom which will cover Acceleration whilst the Uma is in Positions 3, 4, and 6, each of which a Late Surger is likely to be.
Leap Forward / Lightning Surge should be taken on all back-liners. This Skill is effectively near-instant Acceleration Skills provided Players put in some additional effort into ensuring the Uma will be faster than the Uma in front. This is achieved by stacking Final Corner Skills such as Shadow Break , Nemesis , or any other randomly activating Corner Skill. Shooting Star can enable an early activation of Leap Forward as well due to its Current Speed bonus.
Slick Surge / On Your Left! will be the most common secondary Acceleration Skill for Late Surgers. It will serve as a backup Accel in case Let's Pump Some Iron! and Budding Blossom miss, and may sometimes be an additional activation for Leap Forward / Lightning Surge .
WARNING
Updrafters is not recommended. Unlike Slick Surge / On Your Left! , this skill was not buffed during the July 1st Patch . The Skill is only effective from 6th to 9th, in which case the Uma will hit Let's Pump Some Iron! + Leap Forward (6th), or are already at a significant disadvantage where the randomness of Updrafters may not save the race.
End Closers
End Closers play very similarly to Late Surgers, their difference being the lack of access to Slick Surge / On Your Left! . Instead, Straightaway Spurt and Louder! Tracen Cheer! are workable as complementary Accel. That said, in the event the End Closer successfully triggers Leap Forward / Lightning Surge , the value of Straightaway Spurt and Louder! Tracen Cheer! are heavily diminished. It may be more economical to work towards activating Leap Forward / Lightning Surge as consistently as possible, rather than spending the effort and SP on these Straight Accel Skills.
TIP
Oguri Cap (Christmas) will typically appear as an End Closer this CM. Players will Inherit After-School Stroll as the 3rd trigger to Festive Miracle for guaranteed Carryover . There are no reliable backup triggers for Festive Miracle .
WARNING
There are no reliable Festive Miracle triggers for Carryover on styles other than End Closers. In these cases, Players will be gambling on completely random Mid-race triggers.
Velocity Skills
In general, Corner Skills are absolutely vital to meet the pre-requisite conditions for almost all Acceleration Skills this CM. Not only do these Skills contribute significantly to fighting for 1st, 3rd, 4th, and 6th for the Inherited Acceleration Skills, but they also contribute to the activation of Leap Forward on backliners.
As such, Skills including Shadow Break , Nemesis , Latch On , and Pumped can be vital for many Uma. To a lesser extent, random Corner Skills may serve the same purpose.
TIP
The halfway point of Hanshin 1600 is precisely the beginning of the Final Corner. Thus any Skill that can trigger after halfway can be a Corner Skill.
Another way to get Velocity on the Final Corner are Downhill Skills. Downhill Speedster is quite effective, albeit very difficult to acquire through Inheritance. Wherever This Wonder Leads Leads as an Inherited Skill will typically not Carryover into the Late-race, but nonetheless it triggers at a critical time that may cause a chain of other skills to activate. Mejiro Dober (Camping) herself will benefit significantly from the Carryover, and has a near guaranteed chance to trigger Leap Forward / Lightning Surge .
Otherwise, any other generically useful Velocity Skill remains effective.

With the support of 6-9* Stamina Sparks, most Players will run 3 SPD , 2 WIT , and Light Hello . This is the most stable strategy to reach a competitive Statline for this CM.
Players may be tempted to run Skill Cards such as Daiichi Ruby which offers Lightning Surge , or Daiwa Scarlet which offers Neck and Neck . If a Skill card is to be used, then it would take the place of one of the SPD Cards.
One particularly notable Card this CM is Taiki Shuttle . On top of strengthened Power Training and an SP bonus, her Event Chain comes with Mile Corners , which can help achieve Acceleration pre-requisites on all Styles. She also has Mile Maven , a very strong Gold Skill for Front Runners and Pace Chasers. All her Hints besides Wet Conditions are also useful for the CM. Overall, Taiki Shuttle is a card you can use on almost every Uma.
Other card options include Nishino Flower for Determined Descent , or Seeking the Pearl for Dauntless and Uma Stan .
Lineage Planning
Front Runner
Front Runners Lineages will look like the usual. Angling and Scheming is the primary Acceleration Skill for Front Runners. The rest of the Lineage will consist of either Mid-race to assist your Front Runner in fulfilling Angling and Scheming ‘s activation conditions, or Skills to help run away with the lead after Accel.
Front Runners do not necessarily have CM18-specific sparks they may want to hunt for. The usual generic Front Runner Sparks such as Groundwork , Dodging Danger , Prudent Positioning , and Ignited Spirit WIT apply. It is recommended to look for Right-Handed ○ or Fall Runner ○ as Groundwork activations well. Other Groundwork activations can come from Taking the Lead and Front Runner Savvy ○ , which are easily acquired from Mihono Bourbon and Light Hello respectively.
Pace Chaser
Pace Chasers have the diversity of choice when it comes to their Lineages. Budding Blossom and Angling and Scheming were explained earlier in the guide.
Whilst the Accel Zone begins on a Downhill, it is not recommended to work Straight Descent into your Lineages. This Skill is too randomly ineffective to afford the effort of Parenting for it.
Head-On is a generically useful Skill to have for all CMs - this can also be acquired from Agnes Tachyon , the Scenario Card.
Late Surger and End Closer
Let's Pump Some Iron! and Budding Blossom are the primary Acceleration Skills for the backline.
Despite the existence of Daiichi Ruby which offers Lightning Surge , it is still recommended to work towards adding Leap Forward to backline Lineages.
All other Unique Skills in the Lineage should contribute towards activating Leap Forward / Lightning Surge as soon as possible. These are Velocity Skills that activate on the Final Corner like Shadow Break and Nemesis , or Skills with Current Speed like Shooting Star . Otherwise, Late-race Skills to help close out the Race remain generically useful.
Recommended Umas
The most competitive Uma this CM are Mejiro Dober (Camping) , Narita Taishin , and Oguri Cap (Christmas) . Each of these three Uma are backliners who have near-guaranteed Speed Carryover, which also translates to guaranteed instant activations of Leap Forward . Consistent bursts of Speed accompanied by consistent Accel is substantially more powerful than what other Uma have access to.
Special Week is similar to the Uma above, however Shooting Star is slightly trickier to activate compared to Nemesis or Wherever This Wonder Leads . Shooting Star's conditions require Special Week to pass another Uma, but this can be difficult without first activating other Skills. Some effort would need to be made for Special Week to pass as early as possible in the Late-race.
Taiki Shuttle (Camping) is strong for different reasons. Mile Maven , Neck and Neck , Pace Chaser Corners , and Mile Corners are each innately available to Taiki Shuttle, and each are crucial Skills for success this CM. The availability of these Skills also dramatically increase her Deck flexibility, making her one of the easiest Uma to train amongst the available roster. Joyful Voyage! is also a powerful Late-race Velocity Skill that will always prove useful.
Otherwise, most Mile-capable Uma will perform well this CM. Many options such as Fine Motion (Wedding) and Nishino Flower are easy to train due to their inherent Mile-related Skills.
Runaway
Runaways are not effective aces this CM, but they do have significant strategic value amongst the team. Each benefit also comes with some risk.
One of their key uses is providing a consistent means for Uma to activate Skills which require Overtake or Overtake Mode. These are Skills like Shadow Break , Shooting Star , Leap Forward , Acceleration , which may be vital Skills in an Uma’s build.
Runaways can also help set the Pace of the lobby to stretch the pack, preventing congestion. A stretched pack will typically reward Uma with better Mid-race performance and may reduce the frequency of Wide Swings . This can especially help Front Runners, who can pace up to the Runaway to establish a significant Mid-race lead ahead of the mid-pack. In this case, Players must be careful not to block their own Front Runners from activating Angling and Scheming by making the Runaway too competitive in the Mid-race.
TIP
On the other hand, players without a Front Runner in the team comp can use Runaways to block Angling and Scheming from opponents. Take caution that too weak of a Runaway may benefit Front Runners instead.
WARNING
Be aware that if the Player’s own Uma can use the Runaway to their benefit, so can opponents! That said, this is likely an acceptable risk if the Player’s Uma becomes significantly more consistent with the presence of a Runaway - after all, not all opponents will build the same way.
